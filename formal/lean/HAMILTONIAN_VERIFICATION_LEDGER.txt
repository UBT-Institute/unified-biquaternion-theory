# UBT Hamiltonian: formal verification ledger

Status: **partial formalization; full single-Theta Hamiltonian OPEN**.

## Already connected to Lean

- `UBT.GR.VolumeVariation`: four-dimensional determinant and affine rank-one first-jet identities.
- `UBT.GR.VolumePrincipalSymbol`: vanishing common-covector second Fréchet variation for the **specified determinant-density model**, including a field-dependent additive shift.
- `UBT.GR.CompositeActionStationarity`: metric Euler–Lagrange equation follows from composite stationarity **provided** the field-to-metric differential is surjective.
- `UBT.Action.PsiRestHamiltonian`: algebra of the **declared free psi-sector candidate**; not an action selected by UBT.
- `UBT.Hamiltonian.VerificationBoundary`: named bridge theorems for the October 2026 Hamiltonian track.

## Open proof obligations (do not silently promote to theorems)

1. Define the complete biquaternionic/Clifford field Theta, admissible connections, grading and reality conditions in Lean.
2. Specify a *principle-selected* complete single-Theta action, including torsion and both curvature channels; document every free coefficient.
3. Prove the actual composite functional variation, boundary terms and regularity assumptions, without treating metric and Theta as independent.
4. Compute the complete Legendre transform, primary/secondary constraints and Dirac–Bergmann closure for the selected action.
5. Prove hyperbolicity, degree-of-freedom count, positivity and the precise torsion-free GR limit, stating nondegeneracy/surjectivity hypotheses.
6. Formalize any quantitative prediction and distinguish theorem, conditional theorem and empirical test.

## Verification rules

- Every theorem must compile in `formal/lean` under `lake build UBT` and the existing GitHub Lean checker.
- No `sorry`, `admit`, unreviewed `axiom`, or circular restatement of the target as a hypothesis.
- Maintain explicit theorem-to-document traceability and use `#print axioms` for important declarations.
- A green CI verifies the *formalized statements*, not unformalized physics or the truth of a physical model.
- The complete Hamiltonian must remain **OPEN** until all required premises and derivations are independently formalized.

This ledger is a roadmap, not a claim of a complete formal proof.

## Reproducible complementary checks

- Lean: `lake build UBT` plus the existing leanchecker and axiom audit.
- Exact computer algebra: `python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_hamiltonian_restricted_jet.py` checks the cross and pure second derivatives of the specific determinant-jet polynomial with SymPy.
- Deterministic integer cross-check: the same test verifies 25 reproducible seeded examples with exact integer arithmetic; **these examples are not a proof**.
- A symbolic zero expression must be checked against independently written identities, not only self-comparison; any symbolic test failure is blocking.
- Extension to torsionful full Theta dynamics must add separate tests and is not covered by the restricted model.
