# Research-origin evidence ledger — initial reconstruction, 27 October 2026

## Status and limits
This is a **provisional and non-attested** reconstruction. The structured record is [reconstruction_2026_10_27.json](reconstruction_2026_10_27.json). It is not a declaration of legal authorship, exclusive priority, human sign-off, the correctness of a theory, or the origin of every equation. Sources have different evidence strength, and the underlying original of every indexed archive item has not been re-inspected.

1. **Biquaternionic foundations:** repository history describes the author's handwritten 2013–2015 work. An archival index identifies a public biquaternionic-electroscalar post with an embedded date of 18 March 2016; the primary page itself was not re-opened for this pass.
2. **Complex time:** the retrospective history places the author's concept approximately in 2019; a separately dated primary source for the exact formulation still needs to be located.
3. **Research layers and Matrix-like intuition:** the history attributes the Layer 1/Layer 2 programme to the author's research direction. His retrospective account of the separate Matrix-like reality-layer intuition must not automatically be equated with this technical programme.
4. **SU(3) and 3-qubit structure:** the repository history attributes the early direction to author-led research. He reports independently exploring elementary counting and repeatedly contesting AI constructions; these reports remain retrospective pending original transcripts. Later commits establish AI-assisted formal work, not first conceptual origin.
5. **Prime-number directions:** an available author message from **8 August 2025** explicitly requests prime-number and p-adic multiverse multiplexing and a message from 9 August rejects an inadequately supported derivation. Subsequent February 2026 discussions distinguish Layer 2 prime-channel choice from automatically derived Layer 1 stability. Later commits document prime-sector, Hecke and no-go calculations, not first conception.
6. **Return from fiber constructions to covariant tetrads:** the author reports explicitly demanding rejection of a fiber formulation and restoration of the tetrad route; repository history also attributes this architectural decision to him. The exact original instruction and decisive change commit are still to be identified.
7. **Independent rediscovery:** the author recalls recognizing that at least one independently explored idea had already been published elsewhere. The 27 April 2026 discussion explicitly distinguishes independent conception from first discovery for complex time, theta and biquaternions. A 13 July discussion compared UBT with older Hermitian/complex-gravity work, including Chamseddine's 2000/2001 paper and 2004 complex-vierbein formulation. Similarity is not a proof of equation-level identity or copying, and no historical priority is claimed. An eventual record must distinguish original dated work, the closest earlier literature and the date the overlap was recognized.
8. **Hamiltonian, action and causal shared reality:** accessible discussion context records the author's algebraic constraints and repeated return to informational/causal principles. PR #804 documents a later mathematics track, not sole intellectual origin or a complete UBT action.

9. **Information-first principle:** the author reports introducing information as a foundation for UBT dynamics and repeatedly requiring that information-theoretic constraints actually enter candidate actions and Hamiltonians. Mathematical models and assumptions were jointly developed and debated with AI; the earliest original proposal and equation-level contribution chain still require primary transcript review.
10. **Principle of shared reality:** the author demanded observer-consistency as a meaningful principle and challenged proofs that merely mentioned but did not use it. Its operational mathematical models, objections, counterexamples and candidate dynamical constraints developed through iterative human–AI discussion; unique selection of dynamics has not been established.
11. **Observer complexity/capacity:** the author introduced the need to account for what an observer can actually infer given finite capabilities as well as causal reach. The associated information-capacity and Hamiltonian-identifiability models were discussed and mathematically refined together with AI; particular inequalities or computations must not be assigned exclusively without a source-level record.

## Synergistic development as the normal case
A concept can originate as an informal or incomplete human intuition, be mathematically sharpened quickly by an agent, and change repeatedly through human criticism and agent recalculation. Record this as `iterative_human_ai`, identifying evidenced stages rather than assigning a single inventor to every displayed equation. An earlier appearance in a notebook, publication or conversation establishes only what that earlier source actually contains; later refinements remain separately attributable when possible. Neither agent-produced polish nor an author's original intuition cancels the other's contribution. If origin or sequence is not documented, use `undetermined` without speculative filling.

## Evidence protocol
Distinguish original timestamped documents, indexed-but-uninspected originals, inspected Git commit diffs, retrospective repository narratives, personal recollections, and conversation summaries. Link every consequential attribution to its source and describe exactly what the source does **not** prove. A Git author, merge author, or `Co-authored-by` trailer reflects artifact history, not necessarily the originator of an idea. Chronological proximity alone does not prove that a particular prompt caused a commit. Use `undetermined` where attribution cannot be established.

## Recording new contributions
At a significant conceptual milestone or research PR, log a concise event with: date recorded; specific claim/decision ID; question or initiating proposal; human constraints, objections and corrections; agent hypotheses, derivations and implementations; rejected alternatives; verifier scope; direct evidence references; uncertainty; and whether the author personally approved the material. Record substantive negative results and architectural reversals as well as successful derivations. Routine edits need no separate event. Never invent a quote, percentage of contribution, human endorsement, or earlier date.

## Privacy, integrity and release
Do not commit raw private conversations, unpublished commercially sensitive ideas, authentication secrets or personal information to this public repository. Preserve private original exports separately when available; reference only a neutral archive identifier and content hash where permitted. Amend prior interpretations with dated follow-up events rather than silently overwriting history. Research-origin evidence is separate from A/B/C/D AI-provenance tiers and does not authorize changing their map or signing Tier A.
