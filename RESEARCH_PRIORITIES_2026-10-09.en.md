## 5. P4 — torus-shape selection

Current result: **DEFERRED** for the present canonical topology.

Canonical complex time has one compact internal circle. A single circle has a radius
but no rank-two torus shape modulus. Real Minkowski time is not canonically compact.

Therefore square/hexagonal/rhombic theta-lattice minimization is not an active UBT
prediction. A radius-selection problem is legitimate; a torus-shape programme may be
reactivated only after a genuine second compact cycle or rank-two lattice modulus is
derived.
