# UBT Current Status — 10 October 2026

This document is the current public-facing research snapshot. It summarizes
already registered claims and gaps; it does not promote any open research
branch to a completed theory.

## 1. Fundamental action

UBT keeps the one-fundamental-action rule. The registered \(S_\Theta\) family
is not yet finalized. The microscopic configuration space, measure/Jacobian,
pairing, derivative content, independent versus composite connections,
gauge-fixed Hessian, stability, and reduction maps still have to be fixed by
one consistent variational principle.

Primary source: canonical/ACTION.en.md.

## 2. General relativity

The covariant-tetrad geometry is the most mature UBT sector. The central metric
construction, rank-ten tetrad-to-metric map, connection reconstruction,
split-jet nonpropagation, and a conditional local Einstein--\(\Lambda\)
infrared branch are established under their stated assumptions.

What remains open is the unconditional microscopic derivation of the full
constrained Hessian/measure/couplings and the associated global/null-patch
completion from the finalized single action.

## 3. Colour \(SU(3)\)

The algebraic stabilizer, Gell--Mann operator decomposition, moving rank-three
carrier, and the exact constrained \(4\times3\) Stiefel rewrite modulo local
\(SU(3)\) are established on the stated timelike branch.

The exact degree count is
\[
24-9-8=7,
\]
for normalized angular modes, with the radial \(\Theta\) mode restoring eight
real one-biquaternion degrees of freedom.

Full QCD is not derived. The preferred finite-radius target is
\[
\rho_0>0,\qquad
Z_B>0,\qquad
c_V\to0,\qquad
\lambda_2\to0,\qquad
M_\beta^2>0.
\]

The remaining dynamical gates include the finite noncompact HLS phase,
locking-operator flow, Yang--Mills/BRST/Slavnov--Taylor structure, threshold
matching, compatibility with the sharp/GR sector, and autonomous gauge
topological sectors.

## 4. Theta and complex time

Physical
\[
\tau_{\rm UBT}=t+i\psi
\]
is a dimensionful UBT coordinate and is not canonically the Jacobi modular
parameter.

For the free compact-circle heat trace, the Jacobi parameter is
\[
\tau_J=\frac{i\,s}{\pi R_\psi^2}.
\]

For
\[
\vartheta_3(\tau)=\sum_n e^{\pi i n^2\tau},
\]
the natural scalar theta subgroup is generated by \(S\) and \(T^2\) and has
index 3 in \(SL(2,\mathbb Z)\). Generic finite weighted theta-like projections
are not modular or mock-modular by default.

Full interacting UBT modular covariance remains open.

Primary source: canonical/bridges/theta_parameter_separation.md.

## 5. CMB full covariance

The H0/H1/H2/H3 full-covariance likelihood/KL interface is available, but the
UBT-specific H3 theory template is still missing.

A valid H3 calculation needs the constrained scalar perturbation action, the
gauge-invariant map
\[
\delta\Theta\to\mathcal R,
\]
the state prescription, and the frozen primordial covariance
\[
P_{\rm UBT}.
\]

Compact \(S^1_\psi\) alone does not imply a spatial cutoff
\(k_{\min}=1/R_\psi\).

## 6. Torus-modulus selection

For a fixed-area flat \(T^2\), the zeta-regularized massless scalar determinant
is proportional to
\[
{\rm Im}\tau\,|\eta(\tau)|^4.
\]

The square torus is a rectangular determinant maximum but a saddle in full
moduli; the isolated positive bosonic one-loop log-determinant does not provide
a finite global modulus minimum.

A genuine massive/interacting/backreacted
\[
V_{\rm eff}(\tau_{\rm mod},\bar\tau_{\rm mod})
\]
with a certified bounded minimum remains open.

Primary source:
research_tracks/theta_torus_potential/zeta_regularized_shape_audit.md.

## 7. Fine-structure and quantitative predictions

The alpha-related programme contains structural, conditional, and numerical
evidence, but no zero-parameter first-principles derivation of the physical
fine-structure constant is currently established.

More generally, no numerical coefficient calibrated from observation counts as
a zero-parameter prediction of the finalized UBT action.

## 8. Current priority

The highest-value next theoretical tasks are:

1. finalize the single microscopic action;
2. compute the finite-radius Stiefel/HLS colour phase and mixed sharp/GR
   radiative source of the determinant anisotropy;
3. derive the cosmological scalar perturbation Hessian and \(P_{\rm UBT}\);
4. derive a bounded interacting torus-modulus potential.
