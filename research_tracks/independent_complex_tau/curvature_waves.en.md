# Curvature and actual tensor waves: a conditional dynamical branch

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: **DERIVED_WITH_ASSUMPTIONS / OPEN_GAP**. This continues the [wave audit](wave_dynamics.en.md). The starting revision is `d51d5d47428dd228086b5460ddfd2702e9251818`. The independent complex tau proposal remains unchanged. Everything below concerns explicitly specified real spacetime sectors; it is not the full spectrum on complex spacetime plus independent complex tau.

There are two results. A curved homogeneous symplectic branch still has a degenerate normal quadratic action. The existing split-jet MacDowell--Mansouri candidate does have a positive tensor kinetic term, two propagating tensor polarizations and an explicit wave equation. Its gravitational action was already a conditional candidate [U1]; this note computes its perturbations and their split-jet realization, without claiming a new microscopic derivation or observational prediction.

## A curved symplectic family still fails the wave test [L1]

Take the existing symplectic action [U2] with the conventions of the preceding wave note. Use a fixed Lorentz connection, not an unspecified field-dependent functional. On a contractible real patch put

\[
\eta_{ab}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1),\qquad
\mathsf H_{ab}=-2\eta_{ab},\qquad
X^a=T(t)\delta^a_0,
\]
\[
\widehat\omega^i{}_0=\widehat\omega^0{}_i=\beta(t)\,dx^i,
\quad \widehat\omega^i{}_j=0,\qquad
P^0=\dot T\,dt,\quad P^i=\beta T\,dx^i,\quad
\dot T\,\beta T\ne0.
\]

The tetrad is generated by the same field, P=DX. The curvature is generally nonzero: its boost component contains the derivative of beta and its spatial rotation component contains beta squared. This connection is the specified jet connection; it is not silently identified with the physical Levi--Civita connection.

For unrestricted normal variations of the original complex field, define

\[
\delta\Theta=i b_a\xi^a,\qquad
\alpha=-\mathsf H_{ab}\xi^aP^b
=-2\dot T\xi^0dt+2\beta T\xi^i dx^i.
\]
\[
\delta Q=-\mathsf H_{ab}D\xi^a\wedge P^b
=d\alpha-\frac{\dot\beta}{\beta}dt\wedge\alpha
=\beta\,d(\alpha/\beta).
\]

The curvature term is included: the covariant identity is the following, with the same field-space symplectic pairing as before.

\[
d\alpha=\delta Q+\omega(\delta\Theta,\widehat R\Theta_0).
\]

The background is stationary because its Q vanishes on the Lorentz-real slice. Field variations of F enter only beyond quadratic order. Since the normal-to-one-form map is invertible, set

\[
b=\alpha/\beta,\qquad W=F_0\beta^2,\qquad
S^{(2)}=\frac12\int W\,db\wedge db,\qquad
\boxed{dW\wedge db=0.}
\]

This is precisely the previously analyzed degenerate quadratic functional, with a different weight. If W is locally constant it is a bulk boundary term; where dW is nonzero its local solutions have the form below, and are null directions of this quadratic bulk action.

\[
b=d\chi+f\,dW.
\]

For the homogeneous weight the integrated bulk density contains no time derivatives. Thus nonzero curvature alone does not repair this family. This is not a result about all curved connections, field-dependent connections, boundary modes, or the nonlinear gauge quotient. In particular, a fixed connection variation has not been substituted for the full variation of a composite connection. The scalar equivariant curvature rank obstruction in [U3] supplies a separate reason not to mistake a scalar symplectic coupling for the complete Palatini bivector coupling.

## Use the existing curvature candidate, including its chain rule [L1]

The already recorded candidate [U1, U4] uses the composite tetrad

\[
E^a=\frac1{\sqrt{N_0}}
\left[dX^a+\omega^a{}_bX^b+K_J{}^a{}_bX^b+wX^a\right],
\qquad X^2\ne0.
\]

Here the physical Lorentz connection omega, the jet correction and the central form are independently varied as in that candidate. The Clifford curvature-square action has the existing exact decomposition

\[
S_{\rm cand}=S_{\rm HP}[E,\omega;\kappa,\Lambda]
-\frac{\varepsilon_\psi\ell^2}{8\kappa}
\int\epsilon_{abcd}R^{ab}\wedge R^{cd},
\]
\[
\kappa=\frac{g_G^2\ell^2}{2},\qquad
\Lambda=\frac{3\varepsilon_\psi}{\ell^2},\qquad
g_G^2>0,\quad \ell>0.
\]

With compactly supported variations and fixed topology, the Euler term contributes no bulk Hessian. Pointwise surjectivity of the jet variations gives the complete Palatini tetrad equation; the remaining physical connection equation is Cartan's equation. Its spinless nondegenerate solution is the Levi--Civita connection. This uses both occurrences of omega, in curvature and in E.

More explicitly, let Phi map the candidate variables to the Palatini variables. At a stationary Palatini background, the chain rule is

\[
\delta^2(S_{\rm HP}\circ\Phi)
=D\Phi^*\,\delta^2S_{\rm HP}\,D\Phi
+\delta S_{\rm HP}[D^2\Phi]
=D\Phi^*\,\delta^2S_{\rm HP}\,D\Phi.
\]

The last term vanishes by the background equations, not by holding the composite tetrad fixed. Solve the linearized Cartan equation and the gravitational constraints to obtain the tensor action below. The curvature-square notation therefore does not introduce an extra fourth-order bulk tensor mode in this candidate. This conclusion depends on its particular Euler-plus-Palatini decomposition; it is not a claim about arbitrary curvature-square actions.

## Tensor Hessian on a genuine vacuum background [L1]

Choose the positive cosmological sign and an expanding spatially flat de Sitter patch. A finite value of ell does not permit a flat Minkowski vacuum for this branch. Use units with light speed equal to one, proper cosmic time t, and dimensionless scale factor a:

\[
\varepsilon_\psi=+1,\qquad
H=\frac1\ell,\qquad a(t)=e^{Ht},\qquad
ds^2=-dt^2+a^2(t)(e^h)_{ij}\,dx^i dx^j,
\quad h_{ii}=0,\quad \partial_i h_{ij}=0.
\]

The background satisfies the vacuum Friedmann equation. Tensor perturbations obey the linear lapse and shift constraints with their tensor-sector perturbations zero. The exponential parameterization has unchanged spatial volume. From the ADM form of the Einstein action one obtains, up to spatial boundary terms,

\[
K^i{}_j=H\delta^i_j+\tfrac12\dot h^i{}_j+O(h^2),\qquad
\left[K^i{}_jK^j{}_i-K^2\right]_{h^2}
=\tfrac14\dot h_{ij}\dot h_{ij},
\]
\[
\left[\sqrt\gamma\,{}^{(3)}R\right]_{h^2}
=-\frac a4\partial_kh_{ij}\partial_kh_{ij}.
\]
\[
\boxed{S_T^{(2)}=\frac1{8\kappa}\int dt\,d^3x\,a^3
\left[\dot h_{ij}\dot h_{ij}
-\frac1{a^2}\partial_kh_{ij}\partial_kh_{ij}\right].}
\]

The verifier recomputes the spatial Christoffel symbols, Ricci scalar and extrinsic-curvature contraction to quadratic order for both polarizations with an arbitrary Fourier direction rotated to the z axis. Rotational invariance and Fourier decomposition give the general quadratic tensor expression. This reproduces the standard GR tensor result, e.g. [S1, equation (2.27)], within the specified UBT research candidate.

With polarization tensors normalized to norm squared two, the two independent amplitudes satisfy

\[
h_{xx}=h_+,\quad h_{yy}=-h_+,\quad h_{xy}=h_{yx}=h_\times,
\qquad h_{iz}=0,
\]
\[
\boxed{\ddot h_\lambda+3H\dot h_\lambda
-a^{-2}\nabla^2h_\lambda=0,\qquad \lambda\in\{+,\times\}.}
\]

For nonzero spatial momentum the symmetric spatial tensor has six components and four independent transverse/trace constraints, leaving two. Its frozen principal polynomial is the Lorentz light cone below. It describes local propagation, not an exact global constant-frequency mode in expanding spacetime.

\[
-\omega_{\rm freq}^2+|\mathbf k|^2/a^2=0,
\qquad c_T^2=1.
\]

## Kinetic sign, energy and cosmological attenuation [L1]

For each independent real polarization amplitude, the momentum and Hamiltonian density are

\[
\pi_\lambda=\frac{a^3}{2\kappa}\dot h_\lambda,\qquad
\mathcal H_T=\sum_\lambda\frac{a^3}{4\kappa}
\left[\dot h_\lambda^2+a^{-2}|\nabla h_\lambda|^2\right]\geq0.
\]

Thus this reduced classical tensor sector has positive kinetic and gradient energy for the candidate's stated positive coupling. The Hamiltonian depends explicitly on time; the inequality is not a conserved global energy statement or a proof of stability of every complex UBT mode.

To distinguish cosmological attenuation from an inserted damping mechanism, introduce conformal time and a canonical tensor variable:

\[
d\eta=dt/a,\qquad v_\lambda=\frac{a h_\lambda}{\sqrt{2\kappa}},\qquad
v_\lambda''+\left(k^2-\frac{a''}{a}\right)v_\lambda=0.
\]
\[
a=-\frac\ell\eta,\quad \eta<0,\quad \frac{a''}{a}=\frac2{\eta^2},\qquad
h_{\lambda,k}=C_\lambda(\eta-i/k)e^{-ik\eta}
+D_\lambda(\eta+i/k)e^{ik\eta},\quad k>0.
\]

The constants are initial data. At wavelengths much shorter than the curvature scale, the oscillatory amplitude scales as the inverse of a; outside that regime the exact solution approaches a finite constant at late time. The apparent negative pump term in the canonical variable is not a negative kinetic norm. The attenuation comes from expansion, not dissipation or removal of high fractal levels. No Planck cutoff, energy quantization rule, alpha value or independently selected length is obtained. Canonical quantization could be imposed on this tensor action, but deriving its quantum postulates from UBT is a separate task.

## Where the propagating mode resides

An explicit lift makes the architectural limitation visible. Choose a nonzero constant Lorentz-real field on the patch, and put

\[
X^a=\nu\delta^a_0,\quad \nu\ne0,\qquad
w=\frac{\sqrt{N_0}}\nu E^0,\qquad
K_J{}^i{}_0=K_J{}^0{}_i
=\frac{\sqrt{N_0}}\nu E^i-\omega^i{}_0.
\]

These are Lorentz-antisymmetric after lowering the first index. The unused spatial rotation components can be set to zero. The generated tetrad is exactly E. Consequently a tensor perturbation can be lifted with

\[
\delta X=0,\qquad \delta w=0,\qquad
\delta E^i=\frac a2h_{ij}dx^j,\qquad
\delta K_J{}^i{}_0
=\frac{\sqrt{N_0}}\nu\delta E^i-\delta\omega^i{}_0.
\]

The tensor wave therefore resides in the composite geometry encoded by the jet/connection variables; this calculation is not a nonzero propagator for Theta alone. A variable appearing without derivatives in a first-order Lagrangian need not disappear from the physical propagating combinations after another variable is eliminated. Here eliminating the physical connection supplies the tetrad derivative terms. Calling all jet variables algebraic is not sufficient to derive an action for Theta alone. Additional sectors might constrain this lift, but they are absent from the chosen vacuum candidate.

## Conditional comparison with observations

The calculated tensor sector inherits the GR propagation law. Comparing its metric light cone with the observed speed of light additionally assumes that photons couple to the same physical metric; that matter coupling has not been derived here. Under that assumption, its value c_T=c lies within the GW170817/GRB 170817A bound [S2], which depends on the source-emission assumptions in that analysis:

\[
-3\times10^{-15}\lesssim\frac{c_T-c}{c}\lesssim7\times10^{-16}.
\]

The two tensor polarizations are compatible with the absence of strong evidence for additional polarizations in the GWTC-5.0 tests [S3]. This does not exclude every weakly coupled extra UBT mode. The standard expansion term 3H supplies no additional dissipation parameter. No waveform likelihood, source-generation calculation, amplitude fit or fit to the expansion history has been performed. A pure de Sitter vacuum alone does not describe the matter and radiation eras.

For a reference calibration only, use the Planck 2018 flat base-LambdaCDM values [S4], neglecting today's radiation contribution. Restoring c gives

\[
H_0=67.4\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},\qquad
\Omega_m=0.315,\quad \Omega_\Lambda\simeq0.685,
\]
\[
\Lambda=\frac{3H_0^2\Omega_\Lambda}{c^2}
\simeq1.09\times10^{-52}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}},\qquad
\ell=\sqrt{\frac3\Lambda}
=\frac{c}{H_0\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}}
\simeq1.66\times10^{26}\,\mathrm m\simeq5.37\,\mathrm{Gpc}.
\]

Here ell is a cosmological curvature length, not an identified Planck cutoff. These inputs calibrate a free candidate parameter; they are not a UBT prediction or a new cosmological fit. Compatibility inherited from this GR sector does not establish observational agreement of the full complex-spacetime/independent-tau theory.
