# Information-first dynamics and the principle of shared reality

Status: **research proposal**, 2026-10-26; NOT a locked UBT axiom, established ghost cure, unique action, Hamiltonian or graviton. This integrates the recent information-first research (#792) with the Hamiltonian nonuniqueness theorem and the quantum-emergence program.

## Operational definitions

**Minimal shared reality:** for a permissible observer transformation g, a properly transformed experimental protocol and Theta predict the same relational observables (or distributions). Symbolically, O[g.Theta,g.protocol]=O[Theta,protocol]. This is covariance of operational predictions, NOT literal equality of tensor components or an automatic projection onto positive-norm configurations. Covariance alone does not select a unique Hamiltonian: distinct Lorentz-invariant scalar potentials, or distinct Proca masses, pass it.

**Stronger shared reality (unproved hypothesis):** compatibility of observers' records, conditional distributions, projections P_phi, relational correlations and reconstruction channels might impose additional consistency constraints that select the physical solution space and perhaps the dynamics. To be nontrivial, define the observer algebra/channels, precise comparison maps and a criterion stronger than ordinary covariance. No definition of this strength currently follows from the locked UBT axioms.

**Information-first selector (unproved hypothesis):** a physically specified statistical functional built from Theta and/or its composite observables could select equations, kinetic forms or constraints. A slogan such as minimizing Fisher information is insufficient without defining the probability distribution, statistical parameters, positive measure, real/complex time, boundary data, observer transformations and admissible variation class.

## Positivity does not automatically resolve ghosts

Classical Fisher information I_ab=integral p (partial_a log p)(partial_b log p) dmu is positive semidefinite for a normalized positive density. This does not establish positive Lorentzian canonical energy. Nor does it require a preferred coordinate time in every covariant probability formulation. Conversely, the indefinite ambient Lorentz carrier does not prohibit a positive physical inner product *after constraints and gauge quotient*. Distinguish: 1) positive information metric; 2) positive physical Hilbert/phase-space inner product; 3) Hamiltonian bounded below; 4) Lorentz covariance. Each needs its own derivation.

## Maxwell / Proca diagnostic, NOT a UBT derivation

Using eta=(-,+,+,+), let F_mn=partial_m Z_n-partial_n Z_m and select for a benchmark L=-F_mn F^mn/4 -m^2 Z_m Z^m/2. Euler–Lagrange equations are partial_m F^mn -m^2 Z^n=0. Their divergence implies m^2 partial_n Z^n=0. For m>0, a plane wave with k^2=-m^2 obeys k.epsilon=0. In its rest frame k=(m,0,0,0), the allowed polarizations are three independent spatial vectors with positive Euclidean norm. For m=0, null k, Lorenz gauge k.epsilon=0 leaves a three-dimensional space containing the gauge vector epsilon proportional to k; quotienting by that null gauge direction gives two physical Maxwell polarizations. The sign of mass-shell and equation is consistent with the stated (-,+,+,+) convention.

This example shows how *dynamics plus constraints* can leave positive physical polarizations even if the ambient Lorentz vector quadratic form is indefinite. It does NOT show shared observer agreement directly implies partial.Z=0: for Proca, transversality follows from the assumed field equations; for Maxwell, Lorenz gauge is a gauge fixing. A transversality restriction alone cannot derive Maxwell/Proca equations or establish all Hamiltonian positivity properties.
