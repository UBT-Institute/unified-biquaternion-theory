# Exact positive-Hermitian Theta theorem for Lorentz-real connection response

**2026-10-26. Integrated Fundamental Dynamics continuation of merged #788/#793. Algebraic theorem, NOT a selected UBT action or graviton.**

Write a fixed general complex 2×2 Theta as Theta=H+K, H=(Theta+Theta†)/2 Hermitian, K=(Theta−Theta†)/2 anti-Hermitian. Consider tracefree Lorentz-compatible congruence response L_Theta(a)=a Theta+Theta a† with a∈sl2(C). For a variation of the connection alone, preserving the canonical anti-Hermitian tetrad response requires **aH+Ha†=0**.

Assume **H positive definite**. Set b=H^(−1/2) a H^(1/2). Then b†=−b and tr b=tr a=0, hence b∈su(2). With Q=H^(−1/2) K H^(−1/2) anti-Hermitian, the allowed tetrad response transforms as

H^(−1/2) L_Theta(a) H^(−1/2) = bQ+Qb† = [b,Q].

Both b and Q are anti-Hermitian. Writing Q=i(q0 I+q·σ), b=i β·σ (real coefficients), gives [b,Q]=−2i(β×q)·σ. Therefore:

* if q ≠0 (equivalently Q is not proportional to the identity), the allowed 3-real-dimensional algebra su(2) has an exact **rank-2 image and rank-1 kernel**, with kernel generated by the traceless part of Q;
* if q=0, the image is zero and the kernel has dimension 3.

This proves an *entire open subdomain* rank classification under positive-definite H and nonscalar Q, strengthening the special-witness calculation of merged #793. It does NOT cover H indefinite, singular or zero, general noncongruence two-sided representations, the \(\kappa\) Weyl-dilation component, or constraints that couple all four spacetime directions.

**Implication:** exactly two real anti-Hermitian tetrad-response directions per index can exist in this algebraic branch, but they are not the two physical TT graviton helicities; no Theta-field dynamics, nonflat on-shell metric, dispersion, action or Hamiltonian has been derived. Reality-preserving connection rank at fixed Theta does not select a torsion functional T[Theta]. The next actual bottleneck is to derive the full constrained differential response (δTheta,δA[Theta],δB[Theta],δT[Theta]) and a physically justified evolution law.
