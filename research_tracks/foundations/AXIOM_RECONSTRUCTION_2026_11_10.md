# UBT first-principles reconstruction: axioms before action

**Status:** research program / critical audit, 2026-11-10. **Not** a derivation of fundamental dynamics, algebraic uniqueness, superluminal signalling or quantum closure.
**Principle:** reverse-engineering can generate *candidate* minimal assumptions, but evidence used to choose those assumptions must not later be counted as an independent prediction.

## Why this reset is needed

The current action registry [canonical/ACTION.en.md](../../canonical/ACTION.en.md) explicitly labels the fundamental action family as DEFINED_FAMILY_NOT_FINALIZED. The local central metric/tetrad result is stronger than the dynamical result. The canonical action audit establishes a fixed-background spin current but a flat-affine obstruction in the minimal Palatini + kinetic branch. The residual SO(2) two-carrier quadratic and commutator-square actions have non-GR polarizations or constraints. The newer complex two-carrier real-sharp-metric comparison reopens *kinematic* polarizations but is not an on-shell GR derivation. Existing information-attractor work is a testable proposal, not a derivation. No independent postulated gravitation action may silently become S_UBT.

## Minimal axiom candidates (all are selectable assumptions until proven necessary)

- **A0 — operational observables and consistency.** Real probabilities and detector outcomes; a well-defined state/observable map; mathematical consistency of local evolution. This does not assume an underlying real metric.
- **A1 — spacetime/causality target.** There is an observable 3+1-dimensional low-energy regime with Lorentzian effective causal cones, approximate local Lorentz symmetry and no measurable causal paradoxes. Do **not** insert Einstein dynamics here.
- **A2 — composition and locality.** A specified associative internal product and covariant composition law for neighboring regions; finite-dimensionality and locality are hypotheses, not experimental deductions. State whether they are necessary.
- **A3 — minimal faithful spinorial representation.** Demand a complex two-component spinor representation of Spin^+(1,3) with a compatible conjugation and bilinear map to Lorentz four-vectors. In this selected representation End_C(C^2) ≅ M_2(C) ≅ C⊗H. **Important:** this establishes a *representation equivalence under the added minimal-spinor assumptions*, not that nature uniquely selects biquaternions. Dirac/Clifford and other representations must be explicit comparators.
- **A4 — emergent geometry.** Candidate E_mu = N_0^(-1/2) D_mu Theta and the central anticommutator define the Lorentzian metric only on the admitted classical slice. Outside it permit the full complex-central symmetric tensor and algebra-valued antisymmetric companion. Decide whether a Hermitian/real physical observable map exists; never identify an arbitrary algebra-valued quantity directly with an instrument reading.
- **A5 — action and conservation.** One genuine S_UBT, specified field domain, real scalar density, connection status, symmetry, derivative order, boundary conditions, and measure. Axioms A0–A4 alone do not fix coefficients or select the Einstein-Hilbert term; this is a proven underdetermination.
- **A6 — quantum reconstruction.** Quantization only after a constraint analysis: physical phase space or functional measure, gauge quotient, positive norm/unitarity, microcausality if asserted, Born interpretation, and observable extraction. Phenomenological random perturbations are not derived quantum noise.
- **A7 — empirical correspondence.** GR two-tensor-polarization infrared limit, matter coupling, weak-field gravity and known particle phenomena must be recovered in specified tested domains; extra modes require quantitative, independently testable coupling and dispersion.

No axiom assumes that torsion is nonzero, that spacetime is simulated, that c is exceeded by controllable information, or that a third GW polarization exists. Those are candidate consequences to test.

## Biquaternion necessity — precise theorem target and countermodel

**Conditional theorem target:** given a chosen *minimal faithful complex 2-spinor module* S≅C² of SL(2,C), its complex-linear endomorphism algebra is End_C(S)≅M₂(C)≅C⊗H. Exhibit explicit basis 1, -iσ_k, multiplication and conjugations, plus the spinor-to-vector readout.

**Limitation:** the hypothesis already selects S. Lorentz covariance and the existence of a Lorentzian metric do not alone force an internal Mat₂(C) *fundamental field algebra*: ordinary real tetrad GR and real vector representations are counterexamples. Avoid claiming uniqueness of C⊗H without an explicit class of allowed algebras and proof excluding alternatives. Separate the representation theorem (STD) from an actual dynamical selection theorem (OPEN).

1. A Lorentzian effective cone constrains causal signal propagation in the observed GR regime.
2. Complex time, additional coordinates, phase velocity > c, group velocity > c in dispersive contexts, coordinate speeds, and quantum entanglement do **not** independently imply controllable faster-than-light signalling.
3. A genuine superluminal sector needs: physical characteristic polynomial of gauge-reduced equations; signal front velocity; operational source/detector coupling; initial-value well-posedness; consistent causal ordering (perhaps preferred foliation, explicitly derived) and empirical bounds. A Lorentz-invariant superluminal signalling proposal has serious causality problems unless additional structure is specified.
4. No evidence for superluminal transport is claimed.

## Action-selection decision tree

Start from the exact field space and symmetry representation. Enumerate local independent invariant densities through a specified derivative order, modulo integration by parts, Bianchi and Nieh–Yan identities, field redefinitions and boundary terms. For each candidate:

1. Check dimension, real scalar density, symmetry, regular flat solution and whether the kinetic term is positive after constraints.
2. Vary **all** genuinely composite quantities (E, g, connection, determinant/measure), not only fixed-background Omega.
3. Compute Dirac–Bergmann constraints and reduced Hessian in a background-independent or precisely limited setting; compare two tensor modes in GR regime and account for every additional mode.
4. If spin/torsion is present, derive its source from variation and test the exact flat-affine obstruction in canonical/gr_closure/gap_10tdyn_10d_canonical_action_audit.tex.
5. Require stationary backgrounds (not only off-shell representers), hyperbolic equations, global integrability, and source coupling.
6. Derive limits and *out-of-sample* predictions with parameters fixed independently. Reject/label candidate on failed constraints rather than tuning silently.
7. Contrast with real GR, Einstein–Cartan, metric-affine, and standard spinor QFT null models.
