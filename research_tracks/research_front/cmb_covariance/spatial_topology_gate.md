# Spatial-topology gate for the UBT CMB programme

Date: 2026-10-09  
Status: global-structure audit

## Local FRW does not select global topology

The active cosmological bridge contains the flat FRW local metric
\[
 ds^2=-dt^2+a(t)^2(dx^2+dy^2+dz^2).
\]

That local line element does not determine the global topology of the spatial
slice.  For example, the simply connected space R^3 and a flat quotient
\[
T^3=\mathbb R^3/\Gamma
\]
are locally isometric.  The local Friedmann/Einstein equations therefore do
not distinguish them.

Consequently recovery of the local k=0 FRW branch cannot by itself imply a
cosmic three-torus or any other multiply connected spatial topology.

## Internal compactification is a different question

The canonical internal circle S1_psi is a compact internal coordinate.  It is
not a spatial identification of the observable three-dimensional FRW slice.
Likewise historical internal T3 constructions using additional imaginary-time
directions do not establish a cosmic spatial T3.

## What would count as a UBT topology prediction?

A genuine UBT spatial-topology prediction requires at least one of:
- a global single-Theta regularity/holonomy condition that permits only a
  particular quotient;
- action-level boundary conditions selecting a compact spatial lattice;
- a derived topology-changing or compactification mechanism for physical
  spatial coordinates;
- a global variational principle comparing allowed quotient sectors.

None is presently established in the canonical cosmology branch.

## Implication for P3

H1 and H2 in the covariance programme are legitimate external competing/null
models of ordinary compact topology.  They are not UBT predictions.

H3 may use a topological covariance only after UBT derives a specific spatial
topology and fixes or pre-registers its physical parameters.  Until then H3
must be labelled UNDEFINED rather than filled with a convenient torus model.
