# Cartan torsion selection versus the UBT composite-concurrency equation

**2026-10-26. Exact compatibility gate, conditional on the Hilbert-Palatini/Einstein-Cartan *effective branch*, not a derivation of its action from the fundamental UBT Theta-only axioms.**

Canonical grounding: canonical/THEORY/canonical/canonical_action.tex gives the independently varied first-order Hilbert-Palatini trial action and its Cartan spin-current equation. canonical/CANONICAL_DEFINITIONS.md gives the Gaussian curved representer with V non-null and the metric-compatible contraction equation K_(nu mu rho) V^rho=W_(mu nu), where W_(mu nu)=g_(mu nu)-nabla^LC_mu V_nu. For consistent Gaussian data W is symmetric and W_mu nu V^nu=0.

On any invertible tetrad the Einstein-Cartan algebraic torsion map is invertible (24 torsion components, 24 spin-current components), hence fixed spin-current tau selects exactly one contortion K_EC[tau]. The **necessary and sufficient pointwise compatibility check** with the single-Theta Gaussian representer is

K_EC_(nu mu rho)[tau] V^rho = W_(mu nu).

Equivalently, K_EC[tau]-K0 lies in the 12-dimensional transverse kernel, with K0=(W_mu nu V_rho -V_nu W_mu rho)/V². This establishes *selection of the earlier 12 transverse components* if tau and the independent Einstein-Cartan branch are genuinely derived. It does not establish such a derivation.

**Zero-spin corollary.** If tau=0 and e invertible, Cartan implies K_EC=0 and hence W=0 is necessary for compatibility. Therefore on any Gaussian curved representer with W!=0, a zero-spin Einstein-Cartan branch and the exact same single-Theta concurrency equation cannot both hold. This is a genuine conditional incompatibility. W=0 is a special concurrent-vector condition, not automatically ruled out on all spacetimes.

**Nonzero-spin case.** A known tau is not automatically consistent: its Cartan-selected K must pass the 12-dimensional projection equality above. The matter spin current must be obtained by varying the *same completed single action*, including all metric/composite connection chain rules. Using a separately posited spin source to force the projection would be a new ad hoc assumption.

**Connection-status caveat.** The canonical source itself labels the HP/EC action a conditional infrared branch, not the finalized Theta-only fundamental action. Consequently this compatibility check narrows a possible branch but does not select the canonical universal Hamiltonian, eliminate the eight-real negative kinetic modes, or solve the psi-sector Dirac constraints. In the bare Theta kinetic/potential family without an independent curvature action, the connection equations differ and are algebraic.
