# Route A: can mixed kinetic terms preserve the split-jet gauge quotient?

**2026-10-26. Exact finite-dimensional principal-symbol gate.** Complements `mm_projector_kinetic_shift.en.md`, the established projector rank and existing metric-lock audit. It DOES NOT replace the first-order Palatini connection Hessian or the full composite action variation.

Let v denote an independent *gauge-invariant geometric velocity* (for example a representative after suitable first-order reduction), and w a carrier-orientation velocity generated by the old split-jet shift while the geometric field E remains fixed. Consider the most general real quadratic two-velocity form
L2 = (a/2)v² + b v w + (c/2)w²,
Hess(L2)=[[a,b],[b,c]].
For the original arbitrary shift of carrier orientation at fixed E, the high-frequency shift acts as delta v=0, delta w=dot epsilon. Gauge invariance at principal order demands H(0,1)^T=(b,c)^T=0; hence **b=c=0**. A nonzero mixed term alone cannot preserve the *old* gauge transformation. This is a necessary principal-symbol condition; lower derivatives and boundary terms must also be checked.

If a!=0, a new degeneracy arises when ac-b²=0: L2=(a/2)(v+(b/a)w)² and Hessian null vector (-b/a,1). But that null direction changes the supposedly invariant geometric velocity, and so cannot be identified with the original split-jet shift. Whether it defines a **new off-shell gauge symmetry** depends on the full nonlinear action and boundary conditions; tuning the determinant to zero is not by itself a Dirac first-class constraint. If a>0 and c-b²/a>0 the kinetic form is positive definite, but then the orientation mode propagates rather than being removed by the old shift. This can be healthy in a fully analyzed theory, but it is a different architecture.

**Limits:** In actual MM/Palatini gravity, E is not an independent conventional second-order kinetic variable and omega is first order; identifying a single scalar a with its true canonical Hessian would be wrong. The theorem states an exact *local quadratic selection gate* for any regular reduced sector whose shift acts on w while keeping v fixed. It does not decide full complex Theta/psi, connection, spin-two or SU(3) gluon degrees of freedom.

**Decision:** pursuing route A now requires either a genuinely shift-invariant combined geometric variable for physical matter or a full candidate action with a newly proved gauge generator and complete Dirac reduction. Arbitrary mixed kinetic terms are not a shortcut.
