# Route A: Ricci-eigenline SU(3) proposal fails on Einstein vacuum; Weyl fallback is noncanonical

**2026-10-26.** This follows the existing MM candidate's local Einstein-Lambda stationary solutions and the prior bare-tetrad stabilizer test. No independent observer principle is assumed.

A proposed local curvature-built timelike colour normal would be a unique future unit eigenvector of the Ricci endomorphism R^a_b, defining P_+=n n† G, E_colour=n^(perp). Where a simple timelike eigenline exists, this construction is covariant under Lorentz transformations (after specifying the time orientation). However the MM vacuum gravitational sector obeys R_ab=Lambda g_ab, so R^a_b=Lambda δ^a_b. **All four eigenvalues coincide**. Every timelike direction is an eigenvector: the projector is undefined, not merely numerically unstable. In flat vacuum Lambda=0, curvature tensors vanish and no curvature-derived vector can be selected.

Even supplementing the Ricci tensor by any tensor built algebraically from the metric and curvature on a maximally symmetric background does not solve the selection problem: the background has full Lorentz isotropy, so an equivariant natural vector must vanish. Weyl curvature on special non-maximally-symmetric vacua can define preferred invariant subspaces or principal null directions but need not yield a unique timelike vector; Petrov degeneracies and flat conformally-flat loci obstruct a universal regular construction. These statements do NOT prohibit constructions using curvature derivatives or global boundary data on a restricted class of solutions, but universality is lost.

**Concrete counterexample:** de Sitter/anti-de Sitter (Einstein) vacuum R^a_b=Lambda I4, n1=(1,0,0,0), n2=(5/4,3/4,0,0), both normalized with G=diag(1,-1,-1,-1). Both have equal Ricci eigenvalue, while their P_+=n n^T G projectors differ. No SU(3) frame rotation changes P_+.

**Further gate (route A only):** either accept that a globally defined geometric colour projector is not obtained from the bare gravitational vacuum and instead use an existing independently physical Theta colour normal (requiring its own healthy dynamics), or derive a new geometric invariant that remains regular and unique even at flat/maximally symmetric solutions. Existing curvature algebra does not supply the latter. This is a falsification of the Ricci-eigenline candidate, **not a proof that all geometric SU(3) mechanisms are impossible**.
