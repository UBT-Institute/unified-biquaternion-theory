# A positive spinor-to-vector bilinear, and the locked tetrad gate

**Exact representation theorem, 2026-10-25. NOT a completed spinorial UBT theory, tetrad, Hamiltonian or action.** Follows #751.

Identify a biquaternion Theta with a complex 2x2 matrix. If the fundamental carrier has the **conditional** pure-left action Theta' = S Theta for S∈SL(2,C), define X=Theta Theta†. Then
\[
X'=SXS^\dagger,\qquad X=X^\dagger,\qquad iX\in W_L=i\,\mathrm{Herm}_2.
\]
Thus a single Theta supports a nontrivial composite Lorentz-vector carrier without an independent field. For invertible Theta, X is positive definite Hermitian; its image covers only the future-timelike cone (up to convention), not all Minkowski vectors. There is also non-uniqueness: Theta→Theta U with U∈U(2) leaves X unchanged, so this bilinear alone loses right-frame information; it does not select a unique physical Dirac carrier or torsion source.

For constant S, derivatives satisfy ∂_mu X' = S (∂_mu X) S†. For local S(x), ordinary derivatives pick up ∂S terms; a properly derived induced connection is required. The related candidate e~_mu = i ∇_mu X transforms by congruence when ∇ is so defined. **But this is NOT the locked canonical UBT tetrad** E_mu=N0^(-1/2)D_mu Theta, and replacing E with i ∇X changes the theory. Further, if ∇_mu X is formed solely by ordinary ∂_mu X, its components can form a full-rank tetrad locally for suitable invertible Theta variations, but it may also be degenerate; no generic rank theorem follows from bilinear covariance.

The actual open problem is a factorized/soldered covariant derivative on the single Theta that retains E=DTheta/sqrt(N0) **and** respects its fixed Lorentz reality slice, or a demonstrated gauge-covariant reinterpretation of that slice without changing Axiom C. A nonlinear derivative depending on Theta and Theta† must have its locality, smoothness across det Theta=0, composite-connection reconstruction, spinor representation, first-jet rank, and constraint closure proved. None is selected by this bilinear identity.

Caveat: congruence is a vector representation and transforms with +1 under a 2π spin rotation; the underlying Theta transforms with -1. This is precisely the useful double-cover distinction, not a proof that the full locked tetrad inherits it automatically.
