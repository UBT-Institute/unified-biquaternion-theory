# Double gate for an emergent UBT graviton: local Ward protection AND physical TT accessibility

**24 November 2026 — C_working, L0 exact conditional tests. This is not a proof of an action for the locked single Theta.**

## Gate W: a LOCAL diffeomorphism Ward identity protects a genuine rank-two metric field

Take a real symmetric \(h_{\mu\nu}\) in a flat four-dimensional Euclidean principal-symbol comparator, with parity-even, local, analytic Lorentz/O(4)-covariant self-adjoint quadratic kernel of derivative order at most two. Euclidean calculations analytically continue at the level of algebraic coefficients; they do not establish Lorentzian unitarity. The complete five-coefficient order-two ansatz and the linearized gauge identity are:
\[
(\Gamma h)_{\mu\nu}=a p^2h_{\mu\nu}
+b(p_\mu p^\alpha h_{\alpha\nu}+p_\nu p^\alpha h_{\alpha\mu})
+c p_\mu p_\nu h
+d \eta_{\mu\nu}p^\alpha p^\beta h_{\alpha\beta}
+e\eta_{\mu\nu}p^2h .\tag{1}
\]
\[
\Gamma^{\mu\nu,\rho\sigma}(p)p_\rho\xi_\sigma=0,\qquad
c=d,\qquad \boxed{b=-a,\ c=d=a,\ e=-a.}\tag{2}
\]
\[
(\Gamma_{\rm FP}h)_{\mu\nu}
=a\big[p^2h_{\mu\nu}-p_\mu p^\alpha h_{\alpha\nu}
-p_\nu p^\alpha h_{\alpha\mu}+p_\mu p_\nu h
+\eta_{\mu\nu}(p^\alpha p^\beta h_{\alpha\beta}-p^2h)\big].\tag{3}
\]
\[
\Gamma_{(0)}h=m_1h_{\mu\nu}+m_2\eta_{\mu\nu}h,\qquad
\boxed{\Gamma_{(0)}(p_\mu\xi_\nu+p_\nu\xi_\mu)=0
\Longrightarrow m_1=m_2=0.}\tag{4}
\]

The real Hessian symmetry requires \(c=d\). For the inhomogeneous metric gauge variation \(\delta h_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\xi_\nu+\partial_\nu\xi_\mu\), exact Ward transversality fixes the other coefficients as in (2), leaving only normalization \(a\). Equation (3) is the standard linearized Einstein/Fierz–Pauli massless kinetic operator; on TT fields \(\Gamma_{\rm FP}h=a p^2h\). This uniqueness is for the **specified** two-derivative, parity-even, h-only, local quadratic class, modulo boundary terms. Higher-derivative terms, extra Stueckelberg variables, nonanalytic terms and interactions are distinct cases.

The general momentum-independent local mass operator is (4). Since a local analytic quadratic kernel has a Taylor expansion at \(p=0\), a full off-shell gauge Ward identity rules out its constant part. Thus **under genuine local gauge symmetry, the zero-momentum mass term is not a tunable parameter**. This does NOT prove a positive coefficient \(a\), a GR nonlinear completion, two healthy constrained helicities, a physical pole, or protection against quantum symmetry breaking. The full interacting theory needs corresponding Slavnov–Taylor/Noether identities and suitable regularization.

## Ward transversality WITHOUT locality is insufficient

At \(p^2\ne0\) the off-shell four-dimensional spin-two projector is (5); it is **rank five**, not a count of the two on-shell massless helicities. The nonanalytic kernel (6) is transverse and annihilates pure gauge perturbations, yet it acts as a momentum-independent mass \(m^2\) on its TT image. Its projector depends on the direction of \(p\) and has no direction-independent analytic value at \(p=0\). Hence a mere transverse identity, or conservation of a composite stress tensor, **does not force** the proposed RPA detuning \(\delta\) to vanish. The locality/analyticity of the **full effective metric inverse kernel** matters. A nonlocal low-energy effective action may be physically useful, but then the massless-pole conclusion needs separate proof.

## Gate A: a gauge-invariant effective metric may still be inaccessible from Theta

The current UBT classical composite is the symmetric traceless bilinear (7) of the spatial vector components. On an isotropic classical background \(\bar a=0\) its first differential is identically zero. Therefore **no smooth finite variation of these constituents at that background can induce a nonzero inhomogeneous metric gauge variation at linear order**. This is a restricted statement about a classical local composite map, not an obstruction to a genuinely quantum/nonlocal collective Hilbert-space gauge redundancy.

Even a fully diffeomorphism-covariant *pullback metric* can fail to carry any graviton: take four scalar coordinate fields \(y^a\) with the flat gradient metric (8). Around the invertible affine background, every first-order perturbation is pure coordinate gauge and has zero spatial TT image; indeed the exact induced metric is locally flat. A formally diffeomorphism-invariant parent action or a Ward identity alone does not create physical spin-two states accessible to the fundamental carrier.

By the on-shell pullback chain rule already derived in PR #909, \(H_\Theta=(Dh)^*H_h(Dh)\), so physical TT modes require a **nonzero TT image** of the actual linearized composite map, or else a separate genuinely quantum collective mechanism. Under PR #909's exact single-biquaternion isotropic SO(2)-equivariance hypotheses this classical TT image is zero. The RH automorphic theta correspondence has not derived a new \(Dh\), a physical quotient or a nonlinear collective Ward symmetry that changes this conclusion.

The correct next gate is (9), with \(\Pi_{\rm TT}\) the **two-polarization spatial helicity projector** at fixed nonzero momentum on the physical gauge-reduced solution space (not the off-shell rank-five \(\mathbb P^{(2)}\) from (5)). **Critical distinction:** the \(\Gamma_{\rm TT}(0)=0\) shorthand in the preceding pole draft is meaningful only after specifying an analytic local tensor kernel and a limiting direction/gauge quotient.
