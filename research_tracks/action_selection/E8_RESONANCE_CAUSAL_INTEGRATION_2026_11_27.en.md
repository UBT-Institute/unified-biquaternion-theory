# E8, resonance and causal emergence: integration map

Status: C_working; OPEN physical dynamics. No change to locked axioms or the covariant first-jet metric. This note is a correction proposal, not a canonical status update.

## A — Scoped E8 correction

PR #485, canonical/algebra/e8_no_go_proof.tex, obstruction 3, incorrectly denies order-five algebra automorphisms. In M2(C), set z=exp(2πi/5), D=diag(z,1), and φ(X)=DXD⁻¹. Then φ⁵=id and φ(E₁₂)=z E₁₂, so its order is exactly 5. This does not establish an integral-order-preserving automorphism of any specified lattice. The original third argument is invalid; the broader ring-isomorphism question is not settled by this counterexample.

The standard Z⁸ lattice is not isometric to E8: Z⁸ has norm-one vectors, E8 is even with minimum squared norm 2. Merely comparing Gram matrices of selected bases is insufficient. The faithful Weyl-group-as-complex-algebra-automorphisms obstruction survives: finite subgroups of PGL2(C) are cyclic, dihedral, A4, S4 or A5; W(E8) contains an elementary abelian subgroup of order 2⁸ generated by reflections in eight orthogonal roots, which none of those groups contains. The conclusion is restricted to that action, not all vector-space embeddings or collective symmetries.

D4 = {x∈Z⁴ : Σxᵢ even}. Its dual quotient has representatives 0, v=(1,0,0,0), s=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2), c=s−v. The diagonal glue union ⋃_{g∈{0,v,s,c}} (D4+g)×(D4+g) is the standard E8 lattice: integer coordinates with even sum or half-integer coordinates with even sum. The index over D4⊕D4 is 4. This is additive Euclidean geometry, not closure under biquaternion multiplication or Lorentz boosts. The physical selection of glue remains OPEN.
