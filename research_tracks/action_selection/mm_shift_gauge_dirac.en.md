# P0: exact shift-gauge kernel and Dirac count for the split-jet lift

**2026-10-26. Conditional classical result.** On the non-null split-jet chart, use E=N0^(-1/2)(D_omega X+Q), Q=K_J X+wX. For a fixed omega, the vertical shift δ_e X=e, δ_e Q=-D_omega e leaves E unchanged **off shell**. Consequently the composed MM action, dependent only on E and omega, is exactly invariant. The shift is a real gauge transformation of that action, not merely a degenerate Hessian direction.

## Covariant presymplectic kernel

Let F:(X,Q,omega)->(E,omega), and let Ω_HP be the covariant symplectic form on the Palatini solution space, with all chosen boundary prescriptions. On stationary and linearized solutions, the composite presymplectic current from the previous first-variation identity is F*ω_HP plus the Euler boundary contribution and allowed exact-form ambiguities. For the vertical vector v_e with δ_e E=δ_e omega=0, the contraction i_{v_e}F*Ω_HP=0 identically, including at a boundary **provided there are no additional independently transforming boundary fields or gauge-fixing terms**. The previous suggestion that such a pure vertical shift generically carries a horizon charge is therefore too broad: any nonzero boundary charge must come from extra boundary action/edge fields, an altered lift, or a transformation that changes E or omega. An Euler charge depends on (E,omega), so also vanishes along the strict vertical shift.

This proves a local inclusion ker(dF) ⊂ ker(Ω_comp). It does NOT establish equality of kernels, regular global quotients, or absence of ghosts in the extra complex Theta, torsion, and psi sectors.

## Explicit constrained mechanical analogue with a derivative-dependent field map

Let E=dot x+u, L=1/2 (dot E)^2. Treat E as an independent variable with multiplier lambda:
 L_1 = 1/2 dot E² + lambda(E-dot x-u).
Configuration variables: (E,x,u,lambda). Canonical momenta: p_E=dot E, p_x=-lambda, p_u=0, p_lambda=0.
Primary constraints: C1=p_x+lambda=0, C2=p_u=0, C3=p_lambda=0.
Canonical Hamiltonian: H_c=1/2 p_E²-lambda(E-u).
Conservation C2 yields the secondary C4=lambda=0 (up to sign), hence p_x=0. The pair (p_lambda,p_x+lambda) has Poisson bracket -1; after eliminating it, (p_u,p_x) are first class. Reduced phase-space dimension:
  8 - 2*2 (first-class) - 2 (second-class) = 2,
so one propagating, positive-energy mode E with H_red=p_E²/2. Gauge variations δx=e(t), δu=-dot e(t), δE=0 are generated by the first-class chain; their lift to the original auxiliary variables is only local where split-jet surjectivity holds.

**Scope warning:** this mechanically realizes the necessary lesson about derivative-dependent redundant fields. It does not establish that a genuine UBT microscopic Theta kinetic term shares this gauge symmetry, nor that the GR graviton has only one DOF; Palatini's physical count is separate.
