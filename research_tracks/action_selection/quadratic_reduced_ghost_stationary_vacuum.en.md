```
H2_chi = -p_chi²/(2a) -a |grad chi|²/2 + m_chi² chi²/2.
```
Here m_chi² is the coordinate Hessian of the potential, not asserted to be a positive physical mass squared; the kinetic ghost statement concerns the momentum sign. At a stationary, constant background, chi's stress tensor and diffeomorphism momentum source start quadratically. Consequently linearized ADM Hamiltonian/momentum constraints involve no first-order chi source; in a regular gauge-fixed perturbative treatment the chi canonical pair survives their linear reduction. Its negative quadratic kinetic energy is unaffected by linear gravitational constraints. This shows a perturbative ghost in the particular model under these assumptions.
