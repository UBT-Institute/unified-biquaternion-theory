# Correct gauge-emergence benchmark for the one-Theta colour programme

**Status:** corrected diagnostic.  This file supersedes the earlier stronger
wording that demanded a gauge-invariant adjoint current with a physical
massless one-particle pole.

## 1. Why a gauge-invariant coloured pole is the wrong target

A non-Abelian gauge boson is not an ordinary particle charged under a global
symmetry generated by a gauge-invariant Lorentz-covariant local current.

The Weinberg--Witten theorem states, under its standard assumptions, that a
theory possessing a Lorentz-covariant conserved current cannot contain a
massless spin-\(j>1/2\) particle carrying a nonzero charge of that current.

Therefore a proposed emergent non-Abelian gauge boson cannot simply be a
massless charged bound state created by an ordinary global conserved current
while all Weinberg--Witten assumptions remain in force.

Gauge theories evade the theorem because gauge symmetry is a redundancy, not
an ordinary global symmetry acting on gauge-invariant local states/current
operators in that way.

## 2. Confinement changes the infrared pole expectation

In QCD, coloured gluons are not gauge-invariant asymptotic physical states.

Gauge-fixed gluon propagators can violate positivity and need not admit a
positive Kallen--Lehmann spectral density corresponding to a physical
one-particle state.

Therefore an exact confining UBT colour theory is **not** required to have a
positive gauge-invariant
\[
Z\,\delta(s)
\]
gluon contribution in a physical spectral density at \(s=0\).

The previous criterion
\[
\lim_{p^2\to0}p^2G_T(p^2)=Z>0
\]
is retained only as a diagnostic for a hypothetical deconfined physical vector
channel, not as a necessary condition for QCD.

## 3. What must emerge instead

The correct target is an effective local gauge theory in collective variables.

A successful UBT derivation must produce an effective/1PI action containing a
connection
\[
A_\mu^a
\]
with an exact or emergent local \(SU(3)\) redundancy and, in an appropriate
gauge,
\[
\Gamma^{(2)\,ab}_{\mu\nu}(p)
=
\delta^{ab}
\left[
Z_A(p^2)
(p^2\eta_{\mu\nu}-p_\mu p_\nu)
+
\frac{1}{\xi}p_\mu p_\nu
\right]
+\cdots .
\]

The transverse part must have the correct ultraviolet Yang--Mills behaviour
and the longitudinal/ghost sector must satisfy the identities implied by the
same gauge redundancy.

## 4. Required non-Abelian consistency

At minimum the collective effective action must exhibit:

1. **local redundancy**
   \[
   A_\mu\mapsto
   U^{-1}A_\mu U+U^{-1}\partial_\mu U;
   \]

2. **one common gauge coupling** in the cubic and quartic vertices;

3. **Slavnov--Taylor/BRST structure** after gauge fixing;

4. **correct physical degree count** after constraints/ghosts;

5. **no explicit gauge-boson mass term** in the unbroken colour phase;

6. **the correct ultraviolet beta function** once the derived matter/ghost
   content is included;

7. **confinement-compatible infrared behaviour**, rather than an imposed
   physical massless coloured pole.

Thus an induced background term
\[
\operatorname{tr}F^2
\]
is necessary but not sufficient.

## 5. Weak current loop remains insufficient

The earlier weak-triplet current analysis remains useful:

a perturbative bilinear matter current produces a two-particle
continuum/logarithmic response and does not by itself generate a new
independent gauge redundancy.

So
\[
\text{weak matter loop}
\Rightarrow
\text{background response}
\]
does not imply
\[
\text{new non-Abelian gauge theory}.
\]

A genuinely collective/critical mechanism is still required.

## 6. Weinberg--Witten escape condition

If UBT claims emergent massless gauge variables from the single Theta theory,
it must make explicit which Weinberg--Witten assumption is avoided.

The preferred/standard escape is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the emergent }SU(3)\text{ is a local redundancy, not an ordinary global
current symmetry of gauge-invariant states.}
}
\]

This fits the conditional coset/frame result already found:
\[
g\sim gh(x),
\qquad
h(x)\in SU(3).
\]

But the coset redundancy alone still lacks independent Yang--Mills dynamics.

## 7. Revised nonperturbative target

The Axiom-A-compatible colour problem is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{derive an exact collective effective action whose local redundancy is
}SU(3)\text{ and whose 1PI vertices satisfy Yang--Mills gauge identities.}
}
\]

Possible diagnostics include:
- a collective-field 1PI two-point kernel;
- functional RG flow of the gauge-invariant effective action;
- a lattice/discrete gauge-fixed effective action with BRST-equivalent
  observables;
- Bethe--Salpeter/collective poles only as intermediate gauge-dependent
  variables, not automatically as physical asymptotic states.

## 8. Physical benchmark regime

In the ultraviolet, where QCD is asymptotically free, the derived effective
action must approach perturbative Yang--Mills with eight gauge directions and
the standard transverse short-distance propagator/vertices.

In the infrared, UBT should **not** be forced to maintain a physical massless
gluon pole.  Confinement or a gluonic mass gap/positivity violation is
compatible with QCD provided gauge symmetry and gauge-invariant observables
are treated correctly.
