# Weinberg--Witten boundary for emergent colour in UBT

**Status:** external theorem constraint applied to the current one-field
programme.

## Statement used

Weinberg and Witten proved that, in a theory with a Lorentz-covariant conserved
current, massless particles of spin greater than \(1/2\) cannot carry a
nonzero charge of that current.

Thus a massless composite spin-one state cannot be interpreted as an ordinary
charged bound state of a global \(SU(3)\) current if all theorem assumptions
hold.

## UBT consequence

The following route is excluded as a straightforward derivation:

\[
\text{global SU(3) Noether current}
\longrightarrow
\text{massless charged spin-1 bound state}
\longrightarrow
\text{gluon}.
\]

The viable route must instead make \(SU(3)\) an emergent **gauge redundancy**.
Then the relevant colour current is not an ordinary gauge-invariant global
current acting on physical coloured asymptotic states.

The exact conditional collective-coordinate redundancy
\[
g(x)\sim g(x)h(x),
\qquad h(x)\in SU(3),
\]
is therefore structurally of the right type to evade the theorem.

It remains insufficient dynamically because the associated connection is
composite and lacks an independently derived Yang--Mills kinetic/BRST sector.

## Required declaration for future composite models

Any future UBT composite-gauge construction must state explicitly which
Weinberg--Witten hypothesis is absent.

Acceptable possibilities include:
- local gauge redundancy so that no ordinary gauge-invariant global colour
  current of the forbidden type exists;
- controlled nonlocality/emergence outside the theorem's local-QFT
  assumptions;
- Lorentz symmetry only emerging in the low-energy gauge sector.

The first option is strongly preferred because UBT otherwise aims to preserve
local Lorentz covariance.

## Confinement warning

Exact QCD does not require a gauge-invariant positive-norm massless gluon
state in the infrared.

Therefore confinement-compatible gluon propagators and positivity violation
must not be mislabeled as failure merely because a physical \(1/p^2\) pole is
absent.

The decisive benchmark is the emergence of a consistent non-Abelian gauge
effective action and its gauge-invariant observable content.
