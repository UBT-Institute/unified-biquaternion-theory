# Determinant term breaks the candidate colour SU(3) to SO(3)

**Status:** exact tangent-space symmetry theorem at the timelike pointwise
vacuum of the classified quartic potential.

## 1. Tangent triplet at the scalar timelike vacuum

At the phase-equivalent reference vacuum
\[
\Theta_0=r\,1,
\]
write a transverse biquaternion fluctuation as
\[
\delta\Theta
=
z_1 I+z_2 J+z_3 K,
\qquad
z=(z_1,z_2,z_3)^T\in\mathbb C^3.
\]

The canonical colour tensors on this complex tangent carrier admit the
algebraic \(SU(3)\) action.

For the full pointwise potential
\[
V=V_0+\mu H+\lambda_1H^2+\lambda_2|\det X|^2
\]
with the nonzero-vacuum tuning, the exact Hessian formula implies that the
\(\lambda_2\)-dependent transverse quadratic form is, up to an overall
positive normalization,
\[
\boxed{
Q_{\lambda_2}(z)
=
\lambda_2\,\|\operatorname{Im}z\|^2.
}
\]

Equivalently, the three real parts and three imaginary parts of the candidate
complex colour triplet are no longer treated as one complex-degenerate
multiplet when \(\lambda_2\ne0\).

## 2. Exact stabilizer inside SU(3)

Let
\[
U\in SU(3)
\]
preserve \(Q(z)=\|\operatorname{Im}z\|^2\) for every \(z\in\mathbb C^3\).

For every real vector \(x\in\mathbb R^3\),
\[
Q(x)=0.
\]
Therefore
\[
Q(Ux)=0,
\]
so \(Ux\) must be real for every real \(x\).

Hence every column of \(U\) is real and
\[
U\in SU(3)\cap GL(3,\mathbb R).
\]

Unitarity then becomes ordinary orthogonality and \(\det U=1\), so
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Stab}_{SU(3)}Q
=
SO(3).
}
\]

Thus a nonzero determinant coefficient explicitly reduces the candidate
complex colour symmetry:
\[
\boxed{
SU(3)\longrightarrow SO(3).
}
\]

## 3. Generator interpretation: exactly the earlier 3+5 split

In the standard Gell-Mann basis, the \(SO(3)\) Lie algebra is generated by the
three real-antisymmetric anti-Hermitian matrices
\[
i\lambda_2,\qquad
i\lambda_5,\qquad
i\lambda_7.
\]

These are precisely the quaternion-adjoint spin-1 triplet found in the exact
operator decomposition
\[
8=3_{\rm spin}+5_{\rm quadrupole}.
\]

The remaining five Gell-Mann directions,
\[
\lambda_1,\lambda_3,\lambda_4,\lambda_6,\lambda_8,
\]
are the symmetric-traceless/quadrupole completion.  They do not preserve the
real/imaginary splitting selected by \(Q_{\lambda_2}\).

Therefore the action audit gives the representation-theory split a direct
dynamical meaning:

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_2\ne0:
\quad
3_{\rm spin}\text{ unbroken},\quad
5_{\rm quadrupole}\text{ broken};
}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_2=0:
\quad
\text{the explicit tangent-space breaking disappears.}
}
\]

## 4. Consequence for physical colour

The exact algebraic stabilizer
\[
\operatorname{Stab}(h,\Omega)=SU(3)
\]
remains valid.

But the generic nonzero-vacuum potential currently used in the action-selection
track does **not** possess that full tangent symmetry when \(\lambda_2>0\).
Its pointwise quadratic dynamics only respects the real \(SO(3)\) subgroup.

Thus physical QCD colour cannot be inferred from the algebraic stabilizer
alone.  The five additional generator directions require either:

1. the symmetry-enhanced \(\lambda_2=0\) branch;
2. an infrared mechanism driving the effective determinant coefficient to
   zero;
3. a different action-derived colour sector whose dynamics is genuinely
   \(SU(3)\)-invariant.

## 5. Relation to older quaternion automorphism routes

The surviving \(SO(3)\) is exactly the natural rotation group of the imaginary
quaternion directions (with \(SU(2)\) as its double cover).

This explains why multiplication-preserving / adjoint-only constructions
naturally produced three generators, while the full eight-dimensional
\(SU(3)\) required the additional five quadrupole operators that do not
preserve the full quaternion multiplication structure.

So the old three-generator result was not merely an incomplete calculation:
it is the symmetry naturally retained by the generic determinant-sensitive
action.
