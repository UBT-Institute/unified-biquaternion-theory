# Nontrivial Yang--Mills topology cannot arise from a globally invertible one-Theta rewrite

**Status:** exact topological boundary on the meaning of gauge-field emergence.

## 1. Raw and normalized targets

The raw one-biquaternion value space is
\[
\mathbb C^4\cong\mathbb R^8,
\]
which is contractible.

On the nonzero timelike branch, radial/angular variables give
\[
\mathbb R_+\times SU(1,3)/SU(3)
\cong
\mathbb R_+\times S^1\times\mathbb C^3.
\]

The normalized angular target therefore has
\[
\pi_3=0.
\]

## 2. Consequence for local field redefinitions

A globally invertible local field redefinition preserves the topology of the
configuration space in each regular patch.

Therefore replacing
\[
\Theta
\]
by exact local coordinates
\[
(\rho,Z/SU(3))
\]
cannot create disconnected principal-bundle sectors that were absent from the
one-Theta configuration space.

In particular it cannot create the ordinary \(SU(3)\) instanton classification
associated with
\[
\pi_3(SU(3))=\mathbb Z
\]
at compactified four-dimensional infinity.

## 3. Zeros of Theta do not automatically solve the problem

Suppose \(\Theta\) vanishes at an isolated spacetime point.

On a small surrounding sphere
\[
S^3
\]
the normalized field is a map
\[
n:S^3\to SU(1,3)/SU(3).
\]

But
\[
\boxed{
\pi_3(SU(1,3)/SU(3))=0.
}
\]

Hence the normalized one-Theta data around the zero carry no integer winding
that can be identified with a Yang--Mills instanton number.

Because the homogeneous \(SU(3)\) frame bundle over the coset is globally
trivial, choosing its canonical/global frame section on the punctured
neighborhood also produces no forced nontrivial \(SU(3)\) transition
function.

One may impose an additional winding frame choice by hand, but that winding is
extra gauge-bundle data not determined by \(\Theta\).

## 4. Emergent topology therefore requires a non-invertible step

If a low-energy autonomous \(B_\mu\) path integral includes nontrivial
principal \(SU(3)\) bundles with
\[
c_2\ne0,
\]
then the map from microscopic one-Theta configurations to low-energy gauge
configurations cannot be a globally one-to-one local change of variables.

At least one of the following must occur:

1. the collective map becomes non-invertible in the strong quantum regime;
2. the effective functional integral sums over additional gauge-bundle
   sectors not individually represented by a classical Theta configuration;
3. singular/patchwise collective variables introduce new topological sectors;
4. the microscopic field content/configuration space is enlarged.

## 5. This is compatible with genuine emergence

An effective theory can possess collective degrees of freedom and topological
sectors that are not manifest as elementary microscopic coordinates.

But this is a stronger statement than exact kinematic equivalence.

Thus the HLS programme has two logically different stages:

### Exact rewrite stage
\[
\Theta
\leftrightarrow
(\rho,Z/SU(3))
\]
on the regular timelike patch.

No new topology appears.

### Quantum emergence stage
An autonomous \(B_\mu\) functional measure may acquire a larger configuration
space.

This stage must be derived dynamically and cannot be justified solely by the
Stiefel degree count.

## 6. Instanton topology as an emergence benchmark

A mature UBT colour derivation should eventually demonstrate how the effective
gauge partition function obtains sectors
\[
Q
=
\frac1{8\pi^2}
\int\operatorname{tr}F_B\wedge F_B
\in\mathbb Z
\]
under the appropriate compactification/boundary assumptions.

If the derived \(B\) measure remains restricted to the exact one-Theta
composite frame, then
\[
Q=0
\]
as a bundle topological charge and the result is not the full Yang--Mills
configuration space.

## 7. Consequence for Axiom A

The one-fundamental-field axiom can still be maintained at the microscopic
level.

But if full QCD topology emerges, the low-energy collective description is
necessarily many-to-one/non-invertible relative to the microscopic local field
coordinates.

That distinction should be explicit:
\[
\boxed{
\text{one fundamental field}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{one-to-one collective configuration space at all scales}.
}
\]

This is a testable structural requirement, not a contradiction.
