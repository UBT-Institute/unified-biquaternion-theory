# Holonomic-jet no-go for treating derivative channels as an internal SU(3) triplet

**Status:** exact kinematic no-go for the proposed local finite-jet copy-index
interpretation. This corrects the stronger interpretation of the preceding
second-jet multiplicity note.

## 1. Jet multiplicity is not field multiplicity

Let a single Lorentz-vector field be denoted by \(\Theta^\mu(x,\psi)\). Consider
the three second-order Lorentz-vector differential expressions
\[
\Phi_1^\mu=\Box_4\Theta^\mu,\qquad
\Phi_2^\mu=\partial^\mu(\partial_\nu\Theta^\nu),\qquad
\Phi_3^\mu=\partial_\psi^2\Theta^\mu.
\]

As abstract coordinates on a second-jet bundle these are three equivalent
Lorentz-vector contractions.  However, on the **holonomic jet of one actual
field** they are not three independent fields.

For a Fourier mode with four-momentum \(k_\mu\) and fiber momentum \(p\),
\[
\Phi_1^\mu=-k^2\Theta^\mu,\qquad
\Phi_2^\mu=-k^\mu(k\!\cdot\!\Theta),\qquad
\Phi_3^\mu=-p^2\Theta^\mu.
\]

Thus the twelve formal components of
\((\Phi_1,\Phi_2,\Phi_3)\) are the image of only four complex amplitudes
\(\Theta^\mu\).  The exact channel map therefore has rank at most four, not
twelve.

## 2. Generic copy-space SU(3) does not preserve the holonomic image

For a momentum with \(k^2\neq0\) and \(p\neq0\), split the field amplitude into
a component longitudinal to \(k\) and components transverse to \(k\).

For a transverse component, the copy-space coefficient vector is
\[
t=(-k^2,0,-p^2).
\]
For a longitudinal component it is
\[
\ell=(-k^2,-k^2,-p^2)
\]
up to the chosen metric/sign convention.

A transformation acting only on the proposed copy index,
\[
(\Phi_1,\Phi_2,\Phi_3)\mapsto U(\Phi_1,\Phi_2,\Phi_3),
\qquad U\in SU(3),
\]
preserves the image of a one-field holonomic jet only if it preserves the
distinguished copy-space lines generated by \(t\) and \(\ell\).

A generic \(SU(3)\) transformation does not do this.  The exact verifier gives
an explicit Gell--Mann generator whose infinitesimal action sends a transverse
holonomic channel vector outside the holonomic image.

Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\text{three equivalent Lorentz contractions in }J^2\Theta
\not\Rightarrow
\text{an internal }SU(3)\text{ acting on independent colour fields}.
}
\]

## 3. Equivalent point-symmetry statement

A genuine ordinary internal symmetry of one fundamental field is a vertical
transformation of its value bundle,
\[
\Theta(x)\mapsto R(x)\Theta(x),
\]
with its action on jets fixed by prolongation.

For constant \(R\), all derivative expressions transform with the **same**
\(R\); prolongation does not rotate
\(\Box\Theta\), \(\partial(\partial\!\cdot\!\Theta)\), and
\(\partial_\psi^2\Theta\) into one another.

Consequently an arbitrary \(SU(3)\) acting on the derivative-channel label is
not the prolongation of an internal point symmetry of the original
biquaternionic field.

One could define a derivative-dependent generalized symmetry, but that is a
different and much stronger structure.  It must preserve the contact ideal /
holonomic-jet constraints and be selected by the action; it is not the
Standard-Model local internal gauge symmetry by default.

## 4. Quadratic second-jet action collapses to ordinary longitudinal/transverse structures

Write formally
\[
L_2=
K_{AB}\,\overline{\Phi_A^\mu}\Phi_{B\mu},
\qquad K=K^\dagger.
\]

In Fourier space define
\[
O_1=k^2 I,\qquad O_2=k\otimes k,\qquad O_3=p^2 I.
\]
Because
\[
O_2^2=k^2 O_2,
\]
the effective quadratic operator on the **single** field has only the standard
two Lorentz tensor structures
\[
\boxed{
M^\mu{}_\nu(k,p)
=A(k^2,p^2)\,\delta^\mu{}_\nu
+B(k^2,p^2)\,k^\mu k_\nu .
}
\]

For the displayed three-channel ansatz,
\[
A=
K_{11}(k^2)^2
+2\operatorname{Re}K_{13}\,k^2p^2
+K_{33}(p^2)^2,
\]
while \(B\) is a linear combination of \(k^2\) and \(p^2\) built from
\(K_{22},\operatorname{Re}K_{12},\operatorname{Re}K_{23}\).

Hence a formal \(3\times3\) copy metric \(K\) does not correspond to nine
independent physical couplings of three fields.  After restriction to
holonomic jets, several entries collapse into the same single-field
longitudinal/transverse operator.

In particular, imposing \(K\propto I_3\) is **not** a derivation of an internal
\(U(3)\) symmetry of the microscopic one-field theory.

## 5. Consequence for the previous multiplicity route

The representation-theoretic statement that \(J^2\Theta\) contains repeated
Lorentz irreps remains correct as a statement about jet coordinates.

The stronger physical inference is withdrawn:

> repeated Lorentz irreps in a finite jet of one field do not by themselves
> furnish independent internal colour degrees of freedom.

This also means that the conditional triplet
\[
(\Box_4\Theta,\ \partial\partial\!\cdot\!\Theta,\ \partial_\psi^2\Theta)
\]
must **not** be used as a Standard-Model colour triplet unless a separate
action-level theorem promotes three independent constrained sections whose
gauge transformations preserve the holonomic/contact structure.

## 6. What routes remain viable

The present no-go does not eliminate all one-fundamental-field emergent-gauge
mechanisms.  The remaining logically distinct possibilities are:

1. **projected/composite bundle:** a rank-three bundle derived from \(\Theta\)
   or its jets, with frame \(SU(3)\) redundancy; already known to be too
   constrained for ordinary perturbative gluons in the simplest projective
   realization;
2. **universal/classifying-map route:** a richer composite map into a
   Grassmann/Stiefel manifold; still must derive healthy independent
   low-energy gauge modes rather than only a constrained composite curvature;
3. **exact auxiliary/collective-field rewrite:** introduce redundant variables
   by an exact transformation of the single-\(\Theta\) path integral and show
   that a local \(SU(3)\) redundancy plus an induced \(F^2\) term follows;
4. **larger fundamental carrier:** an explicit multiplicity such as
   \(\mathbb B\otimes\mathbb C^3\); this would change the current minimal
   Axiom-A field content and is not presently canonical;
5. **nonlocal/spectral emergent multiplicity:** possible in principle, but it
   would no longer be a local finite-jet derivation and requires an independent
   locality/unitarity analysis.

## 7. Revised minimum target

The next colour derivation should no longer search for \(K\propto I_3\) among
three derivative contractions of one field.

The decisive question is instead:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can the single-}\Theta\text{ theory be rewritten exactly in collective
variables with a genuine local }SU(3)\text{ redundancy and healthy emergent
gauge modes?}
}
\]

If not, the minimal canonical field content must be revised before UBT can
claim a first-principles Standard-Model colour sector.
