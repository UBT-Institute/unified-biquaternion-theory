# Double symmetry-enhanced finite-radius colour surface

**Status:** exact effective-sector symmetry statement plus a GR-spurion
boundary.

## 1. Candidate finite-radius surface

Consider the timelike colour/HLS effective sector at
\[
\boxed{
\rho_0>0,
\qquad
\lambda_2=0,
\qquad
c_V=0,
\qquad
Z_B>0.
}
\]

This avoids the singular raw-field origin while removing the two leading
explicit obstructions to unbroken colour:
- determinant-induced \(SU(3)\to SO(3)\) breaking;
- HLS/Stueckelberg locking mass.

## 2. Potential-sector enhancement

At
\[
\lambda_2=0
\]
the pointwise potential is
\[
V=V_0+\mu H+\lambda_1H^2,
\]
with
\[
H=2h_B(\Theta,\Theta).
\]

Within the isolated fixed-background timelike sigma-model candidate, both
\(H\) and the positive metric \(K_\Theta^+\) are \(SU(1,3)\)-covariant.

Therefore the isolated colour sigma sector has an enhanced global
\[
\boxed{SU(1,3)}
\]
symmetry.

The determinant anisotropy
\[
|\det X|^2
\]
is not \(SU(1,3)\)-invariant.

Hence a regulator/renormalization prescription preserving the isolated
\(SU(1,3)\) symmetry cannot generate an additive \(\lambda_2\) term from
\(\lambda_2=0\).

Schematically,
\[
\boxed{
\beta_{\lambda_2}
=
\lambda_2 F_\lambda
}
\]
inside the exactly \(SU(1,3)\)-symmetric isolated sector.

## 3. HLS-sector enhancement

Once
\[
Z_B>0
\]
has been generated, the zero-locking surface
\[
c_V=0
\]
separates:
- the local Stiefel frame redundancy \(SU(3)_Z\);
- the autonomous Yang--Mills redundancy \(SU(3)_B\).

Therefore
\[
\boxed{
c_V=0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
SU(3)_Z^{\rm local}\times SU(3)_B^{\rm local}.
}
\]

The locking operator
\[
-\operatorname{tr}(B-C_0)^2
\]
breaks this product to the diagonal.

If all other product-symmetry-breaking cross operators are also absent, loops
of the decoupled sectors cannot generate \(c_V\) additively.

## 4. Combined effective symmetry

On the idealized finite-radius colour surface, the effective-sector symmetry
is schematically
\[
\boxed{
SU(1,3)_{\rm global}
\times
SU(3)_Z^{\rm local}
\times
SU(3)_B^{\rm local},
}
\]
with the usual quotient/redundancy qualifications of the Stiefel
representation.

This makes
\[
\lambda_2=0,\qquad c_V=0
\]
a doubly symmetry-enhanced point **within the isolated colour EFT**.

## 5. The asymmetry: full UBT does not possess microscopic SU(1,3)

The canonical UBT GR/tetrad construction uses:
- the sharp involution;
- biquaternion multiplication;
- determinant identities;
- the Lorentz-real slice.

These structures are not invariant under generic coefficient-space
\(SU(1,3)\).

Therefore the full microscopic theory does not protect
\[
\lambda_2=0
\]
by exact \(SU(1,3)\).

The GR/sharp sector acts as an explicit symmetry-breaking spurion for the
isolated colour sigma model.

## 6. Spurion form of the determinant beta function

Let collectively
\[
\epsilon_\sharp
\]
denote couplings/operators that distinguish the sharp/determinant UBT core
from the isolated \(SU(1,3)\)-symmetric colour sector.

Then symmetry permits the schematic structure
\[
\boxed{
\beta_{\lambda_2}
=
\lambda_2 F_\lambda
+
\epsilon_\sharp\,G_\lambda
+\cdots .
}
\]

Thus
\[
\lambda_2=0
\]
is radiatively stable only if the relevant \(SU(1,3)\)-breaking spurions vanish
or decouple in the colour regime.

This is now the sharp form of the GR--colour compatibility problem.

## 7. Better hierarchy of blockers

The finite-radius HLS route no longer requires the raw radial field to vanish.

Its leading requirements are:

1. generate
   \[
   Z_B>0
   \]
   in a locked/high-energy regime;

2. reach the product-symmetry surface
   \[
   c_V\to0;
   \]

3. suppress the determinant anisotropy
   \[
   \lambda_2\to0;
   \]

4. ensure the sharp/GR spurions sourcing \(\lambda_2\) are irrelevant or
   decoupled at the colour matching scale;

5. gap the charged frame sector;

6. match to autonomous \(SU(3)_B\) Yang--Mills.

## 8. Primary new calculation

The most informative next quantity is no longer another group-theory
identity.

It is the mixed-sector RG source
\[
\boxed{
\left.
\beta_{\lambda_2}
\right|_{\lambda_2=0}
}
\]
generated by the canonical sharp/tetrad interactions.

If it is nonzero and unsuppressed, the finite-radius unbroken-colour surface is
not radiatively stable.

If it vanishes or is irrelevant after colour/GR scale separation, the
double-enhancement route becomes substantially stronger.

## 9. Status

Within the isolated colour EFT:
\[
\boxed{
\lambda_2=0,\ c_V=0
}
\]
is symmetry enhanced.

Within full canonical UBT:
- \(c_V=0\) can be technically protected after autonomous \(B\)-dynamics
  emerges;
- \(\lambda_2=0\) remains conditional on decoupling/suppression of the
  sharp-sector spurions.

This difference must remain explicit in future claims.
