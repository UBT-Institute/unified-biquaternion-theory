# Induced-coupling stress test for the SU(1,3)/SU(3) coset route

**Status:** conditional one-loop benchmark.  The coefficient below follows from
the standard Laplace-type heat kernel for the minimal charged coset sector.
It is not yet a prediction of \(g_s\).

## 1. Charged coset content

For
\[
SU(1,3)/SU(3),
\]
the seven real coset directions decompose under the stabilizer as
\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak m_{\mathbb R}
\cong
\mathbf3_{\mathbb C}\oplus\mathbf1_{\mathbb R}.
}
\]

The six off-diagonal real generators are one complex fundamental triplet of
\(SU(3)\).  The relative phase direction is an \(SU(3)\) singlet.

Thus the minimal charged fluctuation content of the exact coset rewrite is one
complex scalar triplet, provided the gauge-fixed Euclidean Hessian on these
modes is of minimal Laplace type.

## 2. Universal heat-kernel contribution

Use anti-Hermitian connection generators
\[
t_a=-iT_a,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf3}(T_aT_b)
=
\frac12\delta_{ab}.
\]

For
\[
P=-(D^2+\mathcal E)+m^2,
\]
the connection curvature is
\[
\Omega_{\mu\nu}=F_{\mu\nu}^a t_a,
\]
so
\[
\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf3}
(\Omega_{\mu\nu}\Omega^{\mu\nu})
=
-\frac12F_{\mu\nu}^aF^{a\mu\nu}.
\]

For one complex bosonic triplet,
\[
\Gamma_1=\operatorname{Tr}\ln P.
\]

The standard \(a_4\) coefficient gives
\[
\Gamma_1
\supset
\frac{1}{24(4\pi)^2}
I_0(\Lambda,m)
\int d^4x\,
F_{\mu\nu}^aF^{a\mu\nu},
\]
where
\[
I_0(\Lambda,m)
=
\int_{\Lambda^{-2}}^\infty
\frac{ds}{s}\,e^{-m^2s}.
\]

For \(\Lambda\gg m>0\),
\[
I_0(\Lambda,m)
=
\ln\frac{\Lambda^2}{m^2}+O(1).
\]

Matching to
\[
S_{\rm YM}
=
\frac1{4g_{\rm ind}^2}
\int F_{\mu\nu}^aF^{a\mu\nu}
\]
gives the conditional benchmark
\[
\boxed{
\frac1{g_{\rm ind}^2}
=
\frac{1}{96\pi^2}
I_0(\Lambda,m)
}
\]
for one minimally coupled complex fundamental triplet.

For \(N_{\rm eff}\) independent triplet-equivalent modes with the same
normalization,
\[
\boxed{
\frac1{g_{\rm ind}^2}
=
\frac{N_{\rm eff}}{96\pi^2}
I_0.
}
\]

## 3. Scale-hierarchy stress test

Ignoring finite threshold terms and setting \(g_{\rm ind}=1\) for orientation,
the required logarithm is
\[
N_{\rm eff}
\ln\frac{\Lambda^2}{m^2}
\approx
96\pi^2
\approx947.48.
\]

Examples:

| \(N_{\rm eff}\) | required \(\ln(\Lambda^2/m^2)\) | \(\log_{10}(\Lambda/m)\) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 947.48 | 205.74 |
| 10 | 94.75 | 20.57 |
| 100 | 9.47 | 2.06 |

Thus one isolated triplet would require an enormous hierarchy to generate an
order-one canonically normalized coupling from zero bare Yang--Mills term.

This is not a theorem that the route fails.  It is a sharp model-building
constraint:

- a derived \(\psi\)/spectral tower could provide large \(N_{\rm eff}\);
- additional charged sectors could contribute;
- thresholds and the actual physical Hessian can modify finite terms;
- the complete gauge/ghost sector must be included after the connection becomes
  dynamical.

But \(N_{\rm eff}\) and the hierarchy must be **derived**, not tuned to fit
\(g_s\).

## 4. Massless case

If the charged coset modes are exactly massless, \(I_0\) is infrared divergent.
Then the one-loop effective action is nonlocal and the correct object is a
running form factor schematically of the form
\[
F_{\mu\nu}
\ln\!\left(\frac{\Lambda^2}{-D^2}\right)
F^{\mu\nu},
\]
rather than a finite local coupling obtained by simply setting \(m=0\).

Therefore a local induced \(g_s\) requires a matching scale, gap, compact
spectrum, or a renormalization prescription derived from UBT.

## 5. Asymptotic-freedom warning

The initial induced term above is generated by charged matter-like coset
fluctuations.  By itself it does not establish the full QCD beta function.

Once independent non-Abelian gauge modes exist, their self-interactions and
ghosts must be included.  Reproducing the Standard-Model running then requires
the complete gauge plus quark/matter spectrum.

So the next benchmark is not merely a positive \(F^2\) term.  It is:

1. derive \(N_{\rm eff}\) from the UBT spectrum;
2. derive the threshold/matching scale;
3. obtain a healthy dynamical gauge propagator;
4. reproduce the sign and coefficient of the low-energy/UV running without
   fitting an arbitrary bare coupling.

## 6. Decision criterion

The induced-only coset route is strongly disfavoured if the finalized UBT
spectrum yields only \(N_{\rm eff}=O(1)\) charged triplets over a modest scale
range and provides no additional normalization mechanism.

It remains viable if a independently derived tower/multiplicity makes
\[
N_{\rm eff} I_0
\]
naturally of the required magnitude while preserving unitarity and the
single-field origin.
