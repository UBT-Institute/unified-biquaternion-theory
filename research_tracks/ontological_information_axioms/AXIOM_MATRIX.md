# Competing axiom programmes and discrimination tests

The objective is **reverse engineering** established physics, not using a religious analogy as a theorem or rediscovering an already assumed GR solution.

| Primitive | Route O (ontological) | Route I (operational) | Route C (causal) |
|---|---|---|---|
| States/events | Nonempty `S` | Preparations, tests, outcome probabilities | Set `E` |
| Relations | Not assumed | Operational equivalence `s~t` | Partial order `≺` assumed |
| Probability | Not assumed | Assumed operational frequencies/probabilities | Not assumed |
| Time | Not assumed | Ordering of laboratory procedures needs justification | Order primitive, duration not yet fixed |
| Space/dimension | Not assumed | Not assumed | Not assumed, unless continuum model imported |
| Composition | Not assumed | Needs independent axiom | Set/poset constructions need justification |
| Light cones | Not assumed | Requires causal/signalling bridge | Emergent only if continuum conditions hold |
| Dynamics | Not assumed | Requires transformation/consistency axioms | Growth/evolution law additionally required |
| Main failure risk | Vacuous / no physical selector | Smuggled-in quantum theory | Smuggled-in Lorentz geometry |

## Priority discriminators

**D1: causal emergence.** Is there a theorem mapping operational composition and no-signalling assumptions to a *nontrivial* event order without choosing a metric? Test with nonlocal box-type, classical and quantum models; no-signalling alone is not an ordering theorem. Initial finite-graph result: [OI-4 theorem and counterexamples](OI4_OPERATIONAL_CAUSALITY.md). **Acyclicity remains an unproved extra axiom**, and indefinite-order quantum processes must not be dismissed.

**D2: dimensional selection.** Try at least `Herm(2,C)`, `Herm(3,C)`, real symmetric matrices, and alternative Clifford modules. List precisely which extra premises eliminate each. The 2x2 determinant identity is a compatibility check, not selection.

**D3: observer-relative information.** Treat different accessible observable algebras as a model, not an assertion of private universes. State a compatibility condition for shared records and examine models in which records fail to match; no inference to supernatural observers.

**D4: action and constraints.** Once the kinematics is chosen, construct a candidate local invariant action, calculate the kinetic Hessian / constraint count, and test for ghosts and unwanted propagating modes. Compare with the existing residual-`SO(2)` and composite-connection audits, rather than replacing those audits with a slogan.

**D5: operational consequences.** Require at least one discriminating, reproducible prediction; Einstein and Standard Model limits are necessary checks, not evidence of a new physical mechanism.
