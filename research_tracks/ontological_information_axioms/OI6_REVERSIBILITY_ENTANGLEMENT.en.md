# OI-6: Continuous reversibility + local tomography is not enough

**Status:** elementary countermodel [STD]; foundational UBT selection [OPEN]. This is a reproduction of ideas and constraints from established GPT reconstruction research, not an original discovery of an information-to-quantum-law theorem.

## A. Assumptions and the countermodel family

For any integer n at least 2, take a single generalized bit with normalized states the Euclidean n-ball. Allow every affine effect with outcomes in the unit probability interval and local reversible rotations from the connected group SO(n). Define an m-party composite by the **minimal tensor product**, namely the convex hull of local product states. This is an explicitly selected composition rule, not a claim about the physical composite of the world.

$$
\Omega_n=\{(1,\mathbf r):\mathbf r\in\mathbb R^n,\ \|\mathbf r\|_2\le1\},
\qquad
\Omega^{\min}_{n,m}=
\operatorname{conv}\left\{\bigotimes_{i=1}^{m}(1,\mathbf r_i):
\|\mathbf r_i\|_2\le1\right\}.
$$

## B. OI-6-T5: All these composites are locally tomographic

For each elementary system use the unit effect and n independently oriented coordinate effects. They all take valid probabilities. These n+1 effects are linearly independent and span the full dual real vector space of the augmented local states. Their tensor products are a basis for the composite ambient dual. The minimal composite has full affine span in its normalization hyperplane because n+1 linearly independent local states give a tensor-product basis. Hence product measurement statistics uniquely separate composite states.

$$
u(1,\mathbf r)=1,\quad
e_j(1,\mathbf r)=\frac{1+r_j}{2},\quad
K_n=n+1,\quad K_{n,m}=(n+1)^m.
$$

**OI-6-T5 [STD, conditional]:** the minimal n-ball composite is locally tomographic for all finite m. The construction works for n=4, where K_n=5 and K_{n,2}=25 rather than the complex qubit values K_n=4 and K_{n,2}=16. A local-tomography axiom alone cannot select a three-dimensional Bloch ball.

## C. OI-6-T6: Continuous pure-state reversibility also holds

Pure states of an n-ball are precisely its unit-sphere points. SO(n) is connected for n at least 2, and for any unit vectors r,s there is a continuous one-parameter planar rotation mapping r to s (choose a perpendicular plane if they are antipodal). Such rotations preserve all local states and the full effect set, so they are reversible.

Every extreme point of the minimal composite is a tensor product of extreme local states. Indeed, all extreme points of the convex hull of a compact generating set must belong to that set; a product with a mixed marginal can be decomposed. Conversely, if a product of pure local states were a nontrivial convex mixture of product states, its pure marginals would force every summand to have exactly those local states. A product of independent local rotations therefore acts transitively on all pure composite states, and the required path is continuous.

**OI-6-T6 [STD, conditional]:** the same construction satisfies pure-state continuous reversibility for all finite composites, even though the composite is defined to have no entangled states. In particular n=3 has exactly the usual local qubit Bloch ball but an entirely separable composite. Even reproducing one qubit locally does not determine global quantum theory.

## D. OI-6-T7: Entanglement is an independent missing ingredient

For any product state with local Bloch vectors r and s, choose two coordinate measurements and the linear correlation witness W. Cauchy-Schwarz bounds its value by one. The same bound holds for any convex mixture of products, hence throughout the minimal tensor. A hypothetical state with zero local means and correlations T11=T22=1 has witness value two and is not in the minimal tensor.

$$
\mathcal W=T_{11}+T_{22}
=r_1s_1+r_2s_2\le\|\mathbf r\|_2\|\mathbf s\|_2\le1
\quad\text{(separable)},\qquad
\mathcal W_{\rm candidate}=2.
$$

The candidate correlations are consistent with local product-effect positivity and no-signalling: for unit local measurement directions x,y and outcome signs a,b they give the following nonnegative probabilities, because the two-coordinate scalar product has magnitude at most one. Thus the candidate belongs to the **maximal tensor product** but is excluded by the explicitly chosen minimal tensor. Including it changes the global theory; it is not generated by the permitted product rotations.

$$
P(a,b\mid\mathbf x,\mathbf y)=
\frac{1+ab(x_1y_1+x_2y_2)}{4}\ge0,\quad
a,b\in\{-1,+1\},\quad
\|\mathbf x\|_2,\|\mathbf y\|_2\le1.
$$

**OI-6-T7 [STD, conditional]:** local tomography and connected pure-state reversibility are not equivalent to entanglement or reversible entangling interactions. The witness is a finite countermodel, not a physical experimental proposal.

## E. Published classification is stronger than this elementary countermodel

Masanes, Müller, Pérez-García and Augusiak classified bipartite systems with Euclidean-ball local state spaces under explicit assumptions of local tomography and continuous reversible dynamics, and found that the entangled member is the standard complex two-qubit model. That is an existing 2014 theorem and must not be claimed as an original UBT result. Separately, de la Torre et al. established a 2012 characterization based on qubit local structure and nontrivial continuous reversible interaction. Masanes and Müller gave wider operational reconstruction results with further axioms such as the subspace axiom; those must not be omitted.

## F. Tests and proof obligations

The file research_tracks/ontological_information_axioms/verify_ball_gpt_countermodel.py contains 10 standard-library regression tests for local effect bounds, exact composite effect rank, parameter counts, connected example rotations, pure product trajectories, separable mixtures, a correlation witness, candidate product-measurement positivity, and n=3 versus n=4 countermodels. Exact rank checks were independently run for n=2, n=3 and n=4. Tests do not prove the general all-m theorem; the argument above does.
