# Nondegenerate tetrad witness versus full UBT stability

This note complements RADIAL_STABILITY_RANK_GATE_2026_11_11.en.md. The healthy auxiliary-connection scalar reduction cannot yield a nondegenerate composite metric, but a distinct **split-jet** kinematic construction already realizes a regular composite tetrad, as documented in research_tracks/action_selection/physics_closure_bare_action_2026_10.en.md.

On a local orthonormal spatial coframe of the closed FLRW construction, the explicitly supplied jet has the matrix e=diag(1,1+T²,1+T²,1+T²) (up to the original orientation and normalization conventions). Hence det(e)=(1+T²)^3 is strictly positive for real T and det(g)=-(1+T²)^6 is nonzero. This is an **exact local nondegeneracy witness**, not a derivation of a stable gravitational field equation.

The original action's sharp/Gram kinetic contraction on nondegenerate tetrads collapses to a volume term. With free split-jet responses and critical-zero field values, all locally representable tetrads can become stationary, including a non-Einstein FLRW witness. In that restricted bare branch the metric has no independent physical kinetic Hessian, so stationarity does NOT imply a bounded physical Hamiltonian, dynamical stability, or GR.

A separate theorem in research_tracks/action_selection/canonical_family_hamiltonian_bridge.en.md, Proposition 9, excludes stationary nondegenerate tetrads for a different **minimal auxiliary SL(2,C) timelike quartic** branch where the composite metric is varied: the connection cofactor variation forces a constant level set of the nonconstant U(rho), and the scalar gradient column of the tetrad then vanishes. This no-go should NOT be generalized to the free split-jet branch or to unspecified full UBT completions.

Research verdict: nondegenerate kinematics EXIST; a stable nondegenerate theory is NOT proved. Next gate: identify a single fully specified constrained action admitting a regular solution, compute the Dirac physical symplectic quotient and its bounded-below Hamiltonian, then check hyperbolic linear fluctuations. Do not add arbitrary curvature or a positive theta spectrum.
