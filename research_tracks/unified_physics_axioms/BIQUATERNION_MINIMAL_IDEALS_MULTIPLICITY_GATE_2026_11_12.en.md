# Minimal ideals versus the full biquaternion regular module

**Exact algebraic gate; no new Standard Model field content is claimed.**

Let A=C tensor H ~= M_2(C), and p=diag(1,0), q=diag(0,1). They are orthogonal primitive idempotents: p^2=p, q^2=q, pq=0, p+q=I. The minimal left ideals Ap and Aq each have complex dimension 2. As left A-modules they are **isomorphic**, not opposite Lorentz chiralities. The regular left module decomposes A=Ap direct-sum Aq; hence it contains two copies of the same defining two-component module.

The commutant of all left multiplications L_a on the full A is exactly the right-multiplication algebra R_b, b in A^op ~= M_2(C). Thus, once a compatible positive Hermitian form is chosen, the unitary right actions contain U(2), including SU(2). However this U(2) acts on the multiplicity/column index of the regular module; it is not automatically weak isospin, and may be constrained or removed by canonical two-sided geometric compatibility and the real Lorentz slice.

Restricting to a single minimal left ideal Ap leaves a complex scalar commutant End_A(Ap)=C; its compact phase subgroup is U(1). The two equivalent minimal ideals **do not by themselves give a Dirac L/R pair**: the right-handed Weyl representation transforms by the conjugate-dual Lorentz action and requires an explicit real/conjugation structure or larger Clifford module.

A concrete matrix proof: for matrix units E_ij, Ap is the set of matrices with only the first column possibly nonzero; Aq has only the second column. Any complex-linear map commuting with all left E_ij acts identically on the two-dimensional row index and arbitrarily on the two-dimensional column index, hence is I_2 tensor M_2(C). Under left SL(2,C), this is also the complex-linear commutant on the regular module.

**Important distinction:** full biquaternion regular module and geometric Theta are not automatically Grassmann fermions. Deriving spin-statistics, a fermionic action, physical chirality, and a dynamical gauge field are independent gates.

Proposed follow-up: (1) identify how the canonical UBT Theta and its sharp/ddagger act on the column multiplicity; (2) calculate the subgroup of right U(2) preserving all geometric constraints and the kinetic action; (3) only then assess an internal U(1) or SU(2) as physical rather than representational redundancy.
