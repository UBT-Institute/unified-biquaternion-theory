# Inverse reconstruction of UBT from established physical laws

**Status:** RESEARCH TRACK / OPEN. 2026-11-10. No fundamental action, physical uniqueness or all-physics closure is claimed.

## The method is INVERSE, not another forward axiom wishlist

Start at *observed outputs* of established theories, infer the **necessary structural constraints** on any common microscopic model, and intersect those constraints. Only then propose a minimal axiom basis; derive forward without importing the output equations. A reconstruction is credible only if it survives competing realizations, assumption-ablation, out-of-sample tests and explicit failure cases. "Reverse engineering" is abductive hypothesis discovery, not a logical theorem of uniqueness without a delimited model class.

### Inverse funnel

1. **Targets O:** collect experimentally tested phenomena and their quantitative domains (rather than taking entire effective theories as axioms).
2. **Invariant consequences C:** translate each O into necessary constraints on observables and maps: Lorentzian IR cone; Hilbert operational statistics; gauge Ward identities and chiral structure; universal gravitational coupling in tested limit; entropy and fluctuation relations; conservation/locality conditions; etc.
3. **Intersection:** identify which constraints are genuinely shared and which are regime-specific; search for conflicts and explicit countermodels.
4. **Minimal premise synthesis:** find small axiom sets A with A => C, using no more structure than necessary; run ablations A\{a_i} and show which target fails. Penalize independent coefficients and assumptions that merely rename an observed field equation.
5. **Selection:** require that UBT (especially C⊗H, full complex Theta, connection and torsion) is preferred over real tetrad GR + standard QFT and other algebraic models by a **new restriction or held-out quantitative prediction**, not just a change of notation.
6. **Forward falsification:** rebuild classical, quantum, gauge and thermodynamic limits from the chosen A. If a required law has been inserted as an axiom, mark it IMPORTED rather than DERIVED.

## Candidate common constraints inferred from the target set (hypotheses, not established implications)

- **I1 Operational probability and compositional consistency.** The theory defines real normalized probabilities and joint tests for composed systems, including interference and entanglement where observed.
- **I2 Observed Lorentzian causal behavior.** The classical/low-energy response has a Lorentzian cone and universal vacuum c; unknown UV sectors must not contradict tested causality.
- **I3 Physical state redundancy.** Local frame and gauge choices do not change observables; the physical phase space is a quotient with constraints.
- **I4 Conserved quantities and consistent dynamics.** Noether/Ward/constraint identities reproduce operational conservation in their tested regimes. Do not assume a Hilbert–Palatini or Yang–Mills action as an unmarked premise.
- **I5 Local low-energy interactions and scale separation.** Recover controlled effective descriptions; if locality is emergent, show its precise domain.
- **I6 Positive physical sector.** No negative probability, negative norm or unstable propagating modes in the measured regime.
- **I7 Empirical universality and coupling.** The same identified physical excitations couple to clocks, light, matter and gravitational detectors as observed, with a fixed parameter map.
- **I8 Thermodynamic accounting.** Irreversibility emerges with an explicit coarse-graining/state rule; information cost does not by itself impose spacetime torison or a new gravitational force.

## The hard inverse problems (where the choice of biquaternions must earn its place)

### G1. Does relativity + operational quantum composition uniquely imply C⊗H?

No known implication from those broad labels alone. With an **additional** postulate selecting the minimal complex two-spinor module S=C² of Spin+(1,3), End_C(S)≅Mat₂(C)≅C⊗H follows. That is a conditional equivalence, not uniqueness of fundamental ontology. Build countermodels using real tetrad GR plus complex QFT, and alternative Clifford/representation frameworks. Discover a genuinely independent operational criterion that excludes comparators before claiming biquaternion necessity.

### G2. Does the same basis select the action?

The action registry canonical/ACTION.en.md is DEFINED_FAMILY_NOT_FINALIZED. Existing kinematics underdetermines Einstein–Hilbert normalization and higher-derivative operators. Compute the full composite variation of the complete complex Theta metric plus connection/torsion, derive the physical Hessian/constraints, and test both existing residual SO(2) mode no-go results and the flat-affine spin-current obstruction. A desired GR equation inserted into the microscopic action is not a derivation.

### G3. Can a complex metric be microscopic but observable outcomes be real?

Allow central complex symmetric and algebra-valued antisymmetric sharp-bilinear sectors off the classical Lorentz slice. Supply a gauge-covariant real-observable map, positive state space, and well-posed causal evolution. Do not require imaginary components or the algebraic bivector companion to vanish; only their unobserved physical effects must reduce appropriately.

### G4. Is torsion mandatory?

The existing pure-pair single-Theta torsion-free no-go is conditional to that branch. Composite torsion provides local kinematic representability, but the newer complex two-carrier branch opens another path. The fundamental inverse targets do not assume torsion: on-shell torsion must be selected by the action, consistent with known spin data and vacuum limits. Spin is not classical rigid rotation.

### G5. Is an extra gravitational polarization possible?

The three canonical pairs found for a restricted commutator-square action are not an observed third polarization. Derive mode eigenvectors, instrument couplings, frequency-dependent antenna response and compare to existing polarization constraints. Network degeneracies do not imply arbitrary invisibility.

### G6. Does the framework entail superluminal signals?

Lorentz symmetry plus c does not demand controllable FTL; complex time or C⊗H do not prove it. Derive reduced characteristic cones, front speed, initial-value well-posedness, operational signalling and causality model before making a claim.

## Reverse-analysis work protocol (mandatory for every candidate premise)

Record: (a) measured target and uncertainty/domain; (b) logically necessary mathematical condition; (c) assumptions used in necessity proof; (d) one countermodel; (e) proposed minimal premise; (f) forward theorem and calculations; (g) which existing law was imported rather than derived; (h) parameter count; (i) held-out experiment discriminating alternatives. Mark OPEN if any item is missing.

**No circularity rule:** choose principles from a training set of physical facts, then validate on independent facts. Do not select a coefficient by fitting GR and call GR a prediction.
