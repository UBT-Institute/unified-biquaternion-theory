# Revised UBT closure strategy: Theta → quantum theory / observer → QED + GR → classical Maxwell / Standard Model

**13 November 2026 — CURRENT PREFERRED WORKFLOW, NOT A NEW AXIOM OR CLAIM OF CLOSURE.**

This note **reorients** CURRENT_WORK_PROGRAM_THETA_FIELDS_GEOMETRY_INFORMATION_2026_11_12.en.md. We previously tried to read ordinary 4D Maxwell field strengths straight from the classical components of Theta and reverse-engineer Maxwell's first-order differential equations. Those exact algebraic/kinematic constructions remain valid comparison tests but are no longer the assumed *fundamental derivation order*.

The new key hypothesis is: the electromagnetic field is an emergent **quantum-interaction sector**, with the macroscopic classical Maxwell equations appearing only after a suitable quantum-to-classical limit, on an effective Lorentzian geometry that also emerges from Theta. The observer and information channel enter at the level of operational quantum states and correlations, not as an anthropic mechanism or an extra classical field.

## 3. Observer/information is part of quantum interpretation, not a Maxwell generator by itself

The pre-existing *conditional* local observer readout
\[

\rho_N=\frac{T_NT_N^\dagger}{\mathrm{tr}(T_NT_N^\dagger)}

A decisive exact identity is
\[
\rho_N[e^{i\alpha(x)}\Theta]=\rho_N[\Theta].
\]
Thus this **single-point normalized readout cannot tell the local complex phase**, nor can it uniquely reconstruct a generic six-component electromagnetic field-strength tensor *pointwise*: its generic real differential rank is only three while the Theta fiber has eight real degrees. This does NOT forbid a gauge-invariant EM field from more complete **between-point correlations, derivatives, parallel transports, Wilson holonomies or quantum operator algebras**, and a local U(1) gauge phase is in fact not itself physically observable.

Prove link transport, local gauge covariance and dynamics rather than declaring the Berry connection of normalized amplitudes to be the electromagnetic potential.
