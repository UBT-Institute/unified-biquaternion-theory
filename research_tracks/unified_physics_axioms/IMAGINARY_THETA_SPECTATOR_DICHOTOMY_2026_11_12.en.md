# Imaginary-Theta spectator dichotomy in the Einstein–Dirac infrared comparator

**Status:** exact conditional functional statement; does not assert the full microscopic UBT action is independent of imaginary Theta.

## Input and established comparator

Use the existing regular Lorentz-real split-jet tetrad e=E(X,K_J,w,omega) with full permitted jet image, and the Einstein–Dirac effective action S_IR=S_G[e,omega,Lambda,C3]+S_D[e,omega,psi] documented in the companion matter-completion note. Write the full biquaternion Theta=X+iY in a chosen real slice. The Dirac spinor psi is independent and Grassmann valued in this comparator.

## Exact spectator shift theorem

Assume **the entire** S_IR depends on Theta only through e(X,K_J,w,omega), with no explicit Y, dY, compact-circle derivatives of Y, boundary Y terms, or constraints on the allowed Y that invalidate local shifts. Then for any smooth compactly supported epsilon,

Theta -> Theta+i epsilon, X fixed, Y -> Y+epsilon

leaves e, omega, psi and S_IR unchanged. Thus delta S_IR/delta Y=0 identically and its Y-Y and Y-other Hessian blocks vanish. Y is a redundant spectator of this **effective comparator** and contributes no independent local physical excitation to it after quotienting by the exact local shift. This is not a statement that the full complex Theta theory has this symmetry; the decomposition and admissibility may be altered by reality, determinant, or global conditions.

The same hypothesis gives an immediate no-emergence result: if all Y configurations yield exactly the same action and observables, no physical Dirac dynamics can be *derived solely from Y* in this comparator. A physical fermionic field must enter as an independent sector or arise from a newly justified, Y-sensitive microscopic structure; Clifford algebra alone does not supply Grassmann statistics.

## Failure of naive full-Theta kinetic repair

The previously computed unconstrained fixed-background minimal Lorentz-pairing kinetic matrix is 2 diag(1,-1,-1,-1,1,-1,-1,-1), signature (2,6). Its invertible velocity Hessian provides no Theta-only primary constraints. Hence simply adding that kinetic term to make Y propagate does not prove a healthy extension; the negative directions require genuine full-action constraints or different derived dynamics. See `research_tracks/action_selection/theta_kinetic_spectrum.en.md`.

## Precise decision tree

A. **Geometry-only IR:** exact Y shift, zero Y physical modes, Einstein–Dirac equations conditionally recovered; not a single-Theta derivation of fermions.

B. **Y-sensitive microscopic action:** shift is broken or modified. Compute full mixed Hessian, Dirac constraint algebra, fermionic Grassmann sector, principal symbol, reduced energy and consistency with the already selected gravity branch. No general positivity claim follows.

C. **Derived fermion from Theta:** requires a specified spinor-valued composite map and a consistent quantum/statistical construction; an ordinary commuting classical biquaternion alone cannot be identified with a Grassmann-odd Dirac field.

## Status

This dichotomy is an exact implication of the explicitly stated comparator, not a new physical prediction or a proof that UBT uniquely reduces to Einstein–Dirac. The choice between A/B/C is OPEN and must be resolved by microscopic input.
