# Bosons and fermions: equal operational entanglement, different exchange statistics

**2026-11-10 | standard finite-mode result + inverse-UBT selection gate, NOT a new particle derivation.**

## Why this note exists

The information principle under consideration concerns **entanglement and shared nonclassical correlations**, not superluminal signal transfer. Both bosonic and fermionic systems can have entanglement. It is therefore incorrect to identify entanglement with fermions alone or interactions with bosons alone. We seek to derive both statistics from minimal physical composition, causality, positive probability and Lorentz covariance, without assuming the microscopic UBT algebra in advance.

## Exact matched-resource dual-rail comparator

Let parties A and B each have two orthogonal modes 0 and 1, and exactly one particle per party. Construct two pure states:
\[
|\Psi_{\rm B}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt 2}
\left(b^\dagger_{A0}b^\dagger_{B0}+b^\dagger_{A1}b^\dagger_{B1}\right)|\Omega\rangle,
\]
where the b operators obey CCR, and
\[
|\Psi_{\rm F}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt 2}
\left(c^\dagger_{A0}c^\dagger_{B0}+c^\dagger_{A1}c^\dagger_{B1}\right)|\Omega\rangle,
\]
where c obey CAR. Use a fixed canonical mode order and define each local logical |0> / |1> by occupying the corresponding mode. Each party has a fixed *local* particle number of 1, and for the fermion case, local fermion parity is correspondingly fixed (odd). Therefore logical qubit rotations and measurements can be generated by **even, parity-preserving local bilinears** c†_{Ai}c_{Aj}, c†_{Bi}c_{Bj}. This avoids misleading comparisons of superselected even/odd local vacuum coherence.

Within the logical tensor-product Hilbert space, the **same** vector
\[
|\Psi\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2
\]
describes both constructions. The reduced logical state for either party is rho_A=I_2/2, so
\[
S(\rho_A)=-\operatorname{tr}\rho_A\ln\rho_A=\ln2,
\]
or 1 bit of von Neumann entanglement.

Logical Pauli X,Z are accessible via parity-even number-preserving local operations. For either realization,
\[
\langle Z_A Z_B\rangle=\langle X_A X_B\rangle=1.
\]
For CHSH, choose A0=Z,A1=X and B0=(Z+X)/sqrt2, B1=(Z-X)/sqrt2; the sum is 2 sqrt2, the quantum maximum. This is an exact finite-mode **same-observable-correlations witness**, not a new UBT prediction or a claim that all boson and fermion protocols are equivalent.

## What still differs?

On exchange of identical particle labels in ordinary 3+1D quantum mechanics, under the usual permutation representations:
\[
P_{12}\Psi_{\rm bosonic}=+\Psi_{\rm bosonic},\qquad
P_{12}\Psi_{\rm fermionic}=-\Psi_{\rm fermionic}.
\]
This exchange-sign statement involves properly defined wavefunctions on the appropriate configuration space; do **not** mistake the mode-swap of two independently addressable logical qubits for physical exchange of identical particles. In 3 spatial dimensions scalar Bose/Fermi exchange statistics follow under ordinary quantum assumptions for identical species; more general permutation representations (parastatistics) require explicit discussion and can be represented under additional structures. Anyonic braiding occurs in 2 spatial dimensions, so "only +/- in every dimension" is not a general theorem.

Another difference is the mode operator algebra:
\[
[b_i,b_j^\dagger]=\delta_{ij},\qquad
\{c_i,c_j^\dagger\}=\delta_{ij}.
\]
The fermionic CAR is isomorphic to a complex Clifford algebra of mode space via Majorana combinations; the bosonic CCR requires a Weyl/Heisenberg structure and a genuinely infinite-dimensional representation for the exact nonzero central commutator. Neither relation is supplied merely by the finite **spacetime** Clifford algebra Cl(1,3).

A fermionic bipartite observable comparison requires respecting parity superselection and graded locality; restricting to fixed one-particle-per-party logical states makes the Pauli measurements legitimately even. Bosonic number superselection similarly matters for some massive-boson protocols and resources. An entanglement entropy by itself does NOT specify the physically accessible entanglement resource if the allowed operations are restricted; the dual-rail example explicitly addresses this issue.

## Inverse selection result and limits

**Exact counterexample (given CAR/CCR and conventional dual-rail encoding):** existence of maximal bipartite entanglement and CHSH violation does **not** single out either Bose or Fermi statistics. Both constructions have rho_A=I/2 and S=ln2, and both attain CHSH=2sqrt2 in the explicitly fixed-number logical subspace.

**What is not derived:** CAR or CCR from information axioms; why spin-statistics applies; fermion or boson field content of UBT; a unique Clifford algebra; the UBT action; any new experimental signal.

**Better target principle:** choose a compositional, measurement-accessible quantum-information structure that includes the exchange operation, its physically realizable braid/permutation topology, local observable algebra, spacetime symmetry representation, spectrum positivity and causal propagation. Then ask whether these inputs and their minimality force ordinary fermion/boson sectors (under the spin-statistics theorem hypotheses) and whether a unified dynamical UBT follows from the same action rather than being inserted.

## Verification

Four exact symbolic logical Bell checks are independently calculated with SymPy: normalization, local marginal, correlations, CHSH. These checks are mathematical tests of the matched fixed-number logical encoding; they are not a field-theoretic derivation.
