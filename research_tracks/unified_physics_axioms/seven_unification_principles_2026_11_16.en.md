```text

Any stronger informational curvature relativity requires new operational predictions and explicit equivalence-principle checks.

## P1. Relativity squared / observer-relative information
Hypothesis: information available to observer O changes its conditional geometric reconstruction g_observer, while shared operational experiments stay mutually consistent. Minimal GR test: compute boosted tidal projections in the same geometry and recover tensor covariance. Novelty test: determine an invariant residual difference after common information and frame transformations; absent a residual, this is epistemic language for standard GR. Avoid a separate fundamental metric per observer.

## P2. Horizon thermodynamics of information
Hypothesis: Einstein-like dynamics is an equilibrium law of microscopic Theta correlations. Established comparator: the local Clausius relation delta Q=T delta S yields Einstein equations under additional assumptions including horizon entropy proportional to area. UBT test: derive horizon entropy, its area coefficient and the effective Newton coupling from a specified Theta state count/correlation measure rather than installing the area law. Entropy alone does not select a unique action.

## P3. Stable universally coupled massless spin-2
Hypothesis: on a genuine on-shell Theta background, constrained linear fluctuations possess a positive-energy massless helicity-2 mode; locality, Lorentz symmetry, gauge constraints and universal coupling force an Einstein-like infrared self-interaction. UBT test: calculate the fully composite gauge-fixed quadratic Hessian and Dirac constraints, poles, residues, 2 physical polarizations, and universal coupling. Standard spin-2 arguments do not produce these assumptions from Theta.

## P4. Fundamental torsion / geometric product
Hypothesis: the antisymmetric biquaternionic geometric sector generates a torsional action whose low-energy combination matches gravity. Comparator: teleparallel equivalent of GR relates the Levi-Civita Ricci scalar to a specific torsion scalar modulo a boundary term, but teleparallel geometry is NOT the generic Einstein–Cartan B architecture (teleparallel requires vanishing curvature of the chosen connection). Test: derive, rather than tune, precisely the torsional combination, admissible connection and spin current; verify the metric limit, consistent torsion background and no ghost modes.

## P5. Spectral principle
Hypothesis: the Theta algebra and covariant transport select a geometric operator D_Theta; a spectral functional Tr f(D_Theta^2/Lambda^2) generates curvature and possibly gauge kinetics. Test: derive operator/domain/self-adjointness or appropriate spectral properties, finite action and coefficients from Theta; show correct Einstein sign and spectrum. Choosing f or D by hand merely reproduces a spectral model rather than deriving UBT dynamics.

<!-- UNIT: p6 -->
## P6. Causal relational transport consistency
Hypothesis: physically consistent composable transport between events and observers constrains allowed Theta dynamics. Identity: U_(gamma2 o gamma1)=U_gamma2 U_gamma1. Caveat: strict endpoint-only, path-independent transport forces flat holonomy and cannot represent generic GR curvature; path-dependent transports are compatible with curvature but composition by itself determines no field equation. Test: introduce only derived transport and test holonomy, causal light cones and a nontrivial action-selection principle.

<!-- UNIT: p7 -->
## P7. Collective stability/emergent gravity
Hypothesis: geometry is a low-energy collective mode of Theta, selected by stable dynamical phases or information organization. Test: find a stationary solution and constrained fluctuation spectrum, identify a universal gapless spin-2 mode, calculate low-energy effective action and cosmological constant, and exclude ghosts, preferred-frame dispersion and extra long-range forces. Existence of collective modes alone is not Einstein gravity.

## Comparison and research priority
P3 (spin-2) is the sharpest diagnostic of the true dynamical sector; P4 (torsion) directly tests the existing algebra and GR program; P2 (horizon information) is a plausible common selection idea but requires a derived state counting. P5, P7 and P6 offer alternate action origins, while P1 already contains a verified GR observer-dependence component and an unproven stronger information-dependent component. Priority is experimental/mathematical tractability, not a probability of truth.
