# Lorentz-spinor centralizer: vector U(1), axial U(1), and multiplicity gates

**Status: exact standard representation-theoretic result, not yet an emergent UBT photon.**

For one complex Dirac module S=S_L direct-sum S_R of Spin^+(1,3), the two Weyl summands are inequivalent irreducible complex representations. By Schur's lemma,

End_{Spin^+(1,3)}(S)=C P_L direct-sum C P_R,

with P_{L,R}=(1 +/- gamma5)/2 (sign convention immaterial). Invertible complex-linear commutant GL(1,C)_L x GL(1,C)_R; phases preserving a chosen positive component norm U(1)_L x U(1)_R. These are internal transformations of the fermion module, not multiplication of the geometric Lorentz tetrad.

Write alpha_V=(alpha_L+alpha_R)/2 and alpha_A=(alpha_R-alpha_L)/2. The free massless Dirac kinetic term has classical global U(1)_V x U(1)_A; the nonzero Dirac mass m(bar(psi_L)psi_R+bar(psi_R)psi_L) requires alpha_L=alpha_R for a continuous symmetry, leaving diagonal vector U(1)_V. Quantum axial anomaly is an additional obstacle to gauging U(1)_A with a single Dirac fermion and generic background gauge fields. Lorentz commutation alone does not establish local gauge invariance, gauge-boson existence, anomaly cancellation, or a Maxwell kinetic term.

For n_L copies of the left Weyl representation and n_R copies of the right Weyl representation, the complex commutant is M_{n_L}(C) direct-sum M_{n_R}(C), and a compatible compact unitary subgroup is U(n_L) x U(n_R). In particular an SU(2) acting on two left-handed copies is possible, but **the multiplicity n_L=2 is extra representation content**; it does not follow from the three quaternion generators or from a single irreducible Dirac spinor. A Standard Model-like weak doublet also needs hypercharges, the right-handed singlet content, Higgs/Yukawa structure, and anomaly cancellation.

For UBT, the current comparator introduces independent Grassmann matter; this calculation identifies the minimal internal symmetry that could commute with its Lorentz spin connection. It does not show that the microscopic single Theta produces this fermion module or its U(1) gauge field. Canonical sharp, W_L, and the reduced two-sided connection remain distinct constraints.
