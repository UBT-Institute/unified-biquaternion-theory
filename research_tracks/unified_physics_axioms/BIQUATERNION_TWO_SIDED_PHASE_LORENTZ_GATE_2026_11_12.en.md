# Two-sided quaternion phases: exact metric covariance and the SO(4) versus Lorentz gate

(E'_mu)^sharp E'_nu=R E_mu^sharp L^{-1} L E_nu R^{-1}=R(E_mu^sharp E_nu)R^{-1}.

Therefore the symmetric central anticommutator metric g_{mu nu} I is unchanged, while the antisymmetric bivector channel Sigma transforms by adjoint conjugation under R. This is an exact algebraic invariant under these hypotheses.
