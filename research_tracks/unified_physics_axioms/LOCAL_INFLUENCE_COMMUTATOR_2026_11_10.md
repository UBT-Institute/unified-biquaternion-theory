# Inverse UBT: explicit operational influence on a three-site quantum chain

**2026-11-10 | exactly verified comparator, NOT an emergence proof for Lorentzian spacetime, GR or UBT action.**

## Problem

Can causal influence be distinguished from entanglement and measured directly from the dynamical algebra, without treating mutual information as metric distance? Construct the smallest nontrivial graph where a distant operation requires multiple interaction steps.

## Three-site calculation

Consider three two-state sites (0—1—2), Hamiltonian with XY nearest-neighbor interactions, \(\hbar=1\):
\[
H=\frac J2[(X_0X_1+Y_0Y_1)+(X_1X_2+Y_1Y_2)].
\]
Define \(A=Z_0\), \(B=Z_2\) and Heisenberg evolution \(A(t)=e^{iHt}Ae^{-iHt}\).
The operational influence diagnostic is
\[
C_{02}(t)=\|[A(t),B]\|_{\rm op}.
\]
Expand:
\[
[A(t),B]
=[A,B]+it[[H,A],B]
-\frac{t^2}{2}[[H,[H,A]],B]+O(t^3).
\]
Direct matrix multiplication in an eight-dimensional Hilbert space establishes
\[
[A,B]=0,\quad [[H,A],B]=0,\quad D:=[[H,[H,A]],B]\neq0.
\]
More precisely \(D^\dagger D\) has eigenvalues \(16J^4\) (multiplicity four) and \(0\) (multiplicity four). Thus \(\|D\|_{\rm op}=4J^2\) for real J, and at small time
\[
\boxed{C_{02}(t)=2J^2t^2+O(t^3)}.
\]
If the 1—2 bond is removed, this commutator vanishes for **all** t since the Hamiltonian acts only on sites 0,1 and cannot reach site 2.

This is a fully specified, experimentally meaningful influence indicator: a local perturbation generated by B can affect the expectation value of A(t) when the commutator is nonzero. Note that a commutator's norm is an upper-witness over states and should not be equated directly to a communication capacity without a protocol.

## General locality / Lieb–Robinson comparator

For sufficiently local bounded interactions, standard Lieb–Robinson theorems establish upper bounds of the schematic form
\[
\|[A_x(t),B_y]\|\le C\|A_x\|\|B_y\|e^{-\mu(d_G(x,y)-v_{LR}|t|)}
\]
with constants depending on the interaction geometry/strength and chosen operator supports. This gives a **soft effective causal cone**. A lattice Hamiltonian generally has exponentially suppressed but nonzero tails outside the cone, unlike exact continuum microcausal spacelike commutation. It is NOT legitimate to identify v_LR = universal physical c without an independent continuum-limit proof, an emergent Lorentz symmetry and a physical length/time calibration.

The graph distance \(d_G\) and site tensor-product factorization are assumed in this comparator; they are **not derived** from minimal information axioms. Therefore the result does not solve the origin of physical geometry or uniquely select complex biquaternions. It works with ordinary finite qubits and generic complex matrix algebras.

## Entanglement does not imply influence

A Bell state shared between sites A,B may have maximal mutual information, while the interaction Hamiltonian between their sites is identically zero. The state remains correlated even though operations on one do not dynamically propagate to the other. Do not identify a pre-existing nonlocal state correlation with an instantaneous controllable signal.
