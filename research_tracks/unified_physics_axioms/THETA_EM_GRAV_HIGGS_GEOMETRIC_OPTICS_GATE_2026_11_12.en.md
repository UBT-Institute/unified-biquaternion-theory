# Theta sectors, principal symbols, and null light rays: conditional bridge

Research status: conditional derivations, not proof of complete UBT unification.

Canonical geometric definitions: E_mu=N0^(-1/2) D_mu Theta and g_mu nu I=(E_mu^sharp E_nu+E_nu^sharp E_mu)/2. Given a smooth nondegenerate Lorentzian metric g, Levi-Civita Christoffel symbols and Riemann curvature follow mathematically; no Einstein dynamics follows from definitions alone.

Matter: represent EM as a complex self-dual bivector / Riemann-Silberstein field, but a projection P_EM[Theta] must be Lorentz equivariant and its first-order Maxwell equation must follow from UBT Euler-Lagrange equations. A complex quaternion has 8 real off-shell components; a complex 3-vector has 6 real components, but component counts alone do not establish a Lorentz-equivariant splitting. A scalar component of Theta is not automatically a Higgs SU(2)_L doublet, nor does it automatically acquire a symmetry-breaking potential.

Geometric optics theorem (conditional): Suppose Maxwell equations dF=0 and nabla_mu F^{mu nu}=0 hold for the metric g, with standard minimal coupling and no nonminimal constitutive terms. Set F_mu nu=Re[f_mu nu exp(i S/epsilon)], k_mu=partial_mu S. Leading order gives k_[lambda f_mu nu]=0 and k_mu f^{mu nu}=0. For a nonzero physical polarization these imply g^{mu nu} k_mu k_nu=0. Since k is a gradient and Levi-Civita connection is torsion-free, k^nu nabla_nu k_mu=(1/2) nabla_mu(k^nu k_nu)=0. Thus rays are affinely parameterized null geodesics in geometric optics. This is NOT a shortest spatial path theorem, and it is not established if Maxwell's principal symbol is modified by Theta couplings.

Wave dynamics: the covariant scalar d'Alembertian is Box_g phi=(1/sqrt(|g|)) partial_mu(sqrt(|g|) g^{mu nu}partial_nu phi). Flat signature (-,+,+,+) gives Box=-partial_t^2+spatial_Laplacian. A Laplacian alone does not encode causal wave propagation. The principal symbol of the linearized Theta Euler-Lagrange operator is the crucial test: for a physical photon/graviton mode its characteristic cone must match g^{mu nu} k_mu k_nu=0, modulo gauge and constraints. Curvature involves derivatives of the connection (typically second derivatives of the metric) and may involve higher derivatives of Theta if g depends on DTheta. Avoid claiming that a wave operator alone yields Einstein equations or massless spin-2 propagation.

Next explicit checks: linearize canonical Theta action about nondegenerate Minkowski background; compute Hessian/principal symbol, gauge null vectors, propagating degrees of freedom and signs; construct equivariant EM and scalar projections; check whether a scalar mode is a true EW doublet and whether gravity has 2 helicity-2 modes. Separate algebraic sector identification from dynamic closure.
