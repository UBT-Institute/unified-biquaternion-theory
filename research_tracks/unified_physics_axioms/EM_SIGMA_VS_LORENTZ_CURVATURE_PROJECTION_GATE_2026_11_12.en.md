# Maxwell field versus tetrad antisymmetric product: flat-background counterexample

**Scope:** conditional on the standard quaternion-conjugation sharp on the real quaternionic basis. The canonical UBT Lorentz slice and sharp must be checked before identifying the illustrative frame with a physical tetrad.

Canonical metric sector: G_{mu nu}=E_mu^sharp E_nu=g_{mu nu} 1+Sigma_{mu nu}, Sigma_{mu nu}=(E_mu^sharp E_nu-E_nu^sharp E_mu)/2.

Choose constant basis tetrad E_0=1, E_1=bold_i, E_2=bold_j, E_3=bold_k, with quaternion conjugation sharp(1)=1, sharp(bold_i)=-bold_i etc. For spatial pair (1,2), Sigma_12=[(-bold_i)bold_j-(-bold_j)bold_i]/2=-bold_k, which is nonzero although all tetrad derivatives and connection curvature vanish in the trivial flat frame. The central symmetric product has diag(1,1,1,1) in this particular real-quaternion convention, so it is Euclidean, not the canonical Lorentzian (-,+,+,+) frame; this is an algebraic illustration only, NOT a counterexample within W_L.

A more robust principle: Sigma is an algebra-valued bivector of the tetrad frame and is generically nonzero even for constant tetrads. It is not automatically an electromagnetic field strength. To claim F_EM=P(Sigma), require (i) a specified Lorentz-real slice and sharp, (ii) an equivariant projection P into real 2-forms, (iii) P(Sigma)=0 in the chosen zero-electromagnetic flat vacuum or a justified nonzero vacuum, (iv) Bianchi dF=0 and sourced d*F=*J from UBT EOM, and (v) no dependence on a chosen Lorentz frame beyond the correct tensor transformation.

One tempting correction is to use derivatives/curvature of the tetrad, but a curvature of the Lorentz spin connection is gravitational and not an independent U(1) curvature by definition. A projection from Lorentz curvature to an abelian electromagnetic 2-form requires a preferred invariant linear functional on the Lie algebra; no nonzero Ad-invariant linear functional exists on the simple Lie algebra sl(2,C) (as real or complex semisimple Lie algebra): if ell([X,Y])=0 for all X,Y and [g,g]=g, then ell=0. Thus no *canonical linear gauge-invariant* U(1) field strength arises simply by taking a linear trace/projection of Lorentz curvature. Nonlinear or symmetry-breaking composites remain open and need explicit proof.

**Verdict:** Maxwell biquaternion encoding is exact, but Sigma and Lorentz curvature cannot simply be relabeled F_EM. An independent matter/gauge construction or a demonstrably gauge-covariant composite with its own dynamics is still required.
