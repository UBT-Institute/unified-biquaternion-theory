# Möbius inversion and the 64-state I Ching analogy: finite dynamical benchmark

**Exploratory / exact finite combinatorics. No physically established I Ching-time correlation. No fundamental UBT action derived.**

Eight upper and eight lower 3-bit trigrams form 64 ordered pairs, equivalent as a *set* to all 6-bit strings. The 8x8 trigram-pair lookup is not a multiplication table: quaternion/bicomplex quaternion/octonion products obey algebraic identities and return products in their algebra; the pair concatenation is only a 64-element encoding. Biquaternions have eight real coordinates and associative multiplication; real octonions have eight real coordinates and nonassociative multiplication. Numerical 8x8 coincidence is not algebraic equivalence.

Take the permutation T rotating a 6-bit word one position. For its n-th iterate, the fixed-point count is F(n)=2^gcd(6,n). The number O(n) of primitive orbits of exact length n is

O(n)=(1/n) sum_{d|n} mobius(d)*F(n/d).

For divisors n=1,2,3,6: F=(2,4,8,64), O=(2,1,2,9). Exactly 2*1+1*2+2*3+9*6=64 states are accounted for. This is a **null benchmark** showing how the Möbius coefficients emerge solely from cycle de-duplication, not from a physical entropy or time law.

For any proposed UBT finite information dynamics T, compute its primitive cycles with the same formula *after* deriving T from the actual Theta evolution. Compare against null models with matched group invariance and cycle statistics. A chosen bit permutation cannot by itself justify Theta evolution, real time, thermal arrow, modular Hamiltonian, or holographic state counting.

Observer access: deterministic bijective T preserves the Shannon entropy of an initial global distribution under mere permutation, but a restricted/coarse-grained observer may have changing entropy. Thus observer entropy and microscopic deterministic dynamics should be modeled distinctly; none of this proves spacetime thermodynamics.

Mertens M(N)=sum_{n<=N} mu(n) is a summatory arithmetic function; it does not enter this exact orbit formula except through further explicitly derived transforms. No link to prime-modulated UBT theta modes or the Riemann hypothesis follows from the 64-state coincidence.
