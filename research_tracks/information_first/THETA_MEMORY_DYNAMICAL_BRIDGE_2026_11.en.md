## Writing a bit requires a physical control coupling

If another accessible mode of the same field supplies a controlled force F(s), the energy becomes V(x)-F(s)x. Solving for a coincident stationary point and inflection point gives the threshold below at which a metastable well disappears. A positive force beyond the threshold favors positive x; a negative force favors negative x. Relaxation while the force is applied, followed by removal of the force, can write the corresponding bit in this comparator. At nonzero temperature or finite duration this is not a zero-error guarantee. The existence, strength and controllability of the coupling and the relaxation mechanism remain physical UBT requirements; the force is not supplied for free by the information principle.

Assume that environmental coarse graining supplies a classical two-state Markov chain with the same constant flip rate r in both directions during storage. Starting in a known bit, solving its master equation gives the probability of reading the wrong bit at time s. This is an endpoint error, not the probability of ever having flipped. The attempt frequency nu, temperature, Markov limit and validity of activation are not fixed by the static Hessian. ## Landauer cost follows from the ideal reset cycle

Slowly raise the gap from zero to Delta_f while equilibrating, then freeze the populations by isolating the logical states and restore the gap to zero. The final error epsilon equals the equilibrium excited population at Delta_f. Integrating the mean force gives the raising work; the return stroke recovers Delta_f epsilon. The total internal energy change is zero, so cycle work equals heat deposited in the bath. This yields the entropy bound without identifying heat with raising work alone. The isolated return and barrier controls are idealized; their nonzero practical costs and finite-time excess must be counted. This protocol illustrates the conditional equality limit, not a universal cost or a derived UBT implementation. Initial side information and correlations require the preceding Landauer ledger.
