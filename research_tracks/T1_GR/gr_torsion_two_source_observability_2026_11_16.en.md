# Is a torsion-mixing coefficient observable? Two-source exact diagnostic

<!-- UNIT: status -->
## Scope [EXACT TOY / CANONICAL APPLICATION OPEN]
This finite-dimensional test responds to the idea that apparent gravitational coefficients may be redundant. V and A are independent **toy** torsion channels with prescribed sources. They have not been identified with the exact 24-component UBT irreducible torsion sectors. In particular b is not assumed to be the canonical Holst or imaginary-biquaternion coefficient. No new fundamental fields are introduced.

<!-- UNIT: formula -->
## Exact elimination
```text
K=(V,A)^T; J=(J_V,J_A)^T; M=[[a,b],[b,c]]
L(K)=J^T K + 1/2 K^T M K
D=det(M)=a*c-b^2
if D!=0:
 Kstar=-M^(-1) J
 L_eff=-(c*J_V^2-2*b*J_V*J_A+a*J_A^2)/(2*D)
if a=c=b=1: M has null (-1,1), solution requires J_A=J_V
```
For D!=0 the algebraic Euler equations yield the displayed contact term exactly. Even for J_A=0, L_eff=-c*J_V^2/[2(ac-b^2)], generically dependent on b. Exact differentiation gives dL_eff/db=-(a*J_A-b*J_V)(b*J_A-c*J_V)/D^2. Unobservability when sources vanish does not imply true redundancy when sources are switched on.

<!-- UNIT: singular -->
## Singular coupling locus
For D=0 the inverse does not exist. Solvability requires source orthogonality to every left null vector. At a=c=b=1 the left kernel is generated by (-1,1), so J_A=J_V is required. A singular auxiliary Hessian can impose source constraints and is not by itself evidence of a gauge symmetry or a new propagating mode.

<!-- UNIT: identifiability -->
## Stronger parameter-identifiability criterion
When the two source components J_V,J_A are independently operationally accessible and their normalization is fixed, the symmetric coefficient matrix of their on-shell contact term is -(1/2) M^{-1}. If two invertible matrices M_1 and M_2 give identical L_eff(J) for **all** two-component real source vectors J, polarization identity implies M_1^{-1}=M_2^{-1}, hence M_1=M_2. Thus there is no nontrivial continuous redundancy among (a,b,c) **under these assumptions**. If only a subspace of sources is physically accessible, only the inverse-matrix restriction to that source subspace can be identified, leaving degeneracies possible. Legitimate source-field redefinitions and an overall classical action scaling must be quotiented separately. This is a toy identifiability theorem, not proof that UBT has three physical torsion parameters.
