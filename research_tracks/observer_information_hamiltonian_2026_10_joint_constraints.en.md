Respect the pre-existing October SU(3) jet-multiplicity no-go and the one-$\Theta$ Lorentz-commutant constraint. For each group verify the representation, local transformation law, connection origin, closure, physical degrees of freedom and anomalies. Do not postulate extra fundamental gauge fields.
