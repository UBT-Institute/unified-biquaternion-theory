# UBT Vacuum Landscape & String Duality — research track
**Status: OPEN / hypothesis-testing; 2026-10-26.** No observed string duality or enumerated UBT vacua are claimed.

## Priority and decision rule
**Priority P0: derive and finalize one genuine Theta-only action, reconstruct its constrained Hamiltonian and prove admissibility.** This track is subordinate: classify global data only after the selected Euler equations and constraints permit backgrounds. Local configurations are necessary test backgrounds, *not* substitute field equations or stability proofs.

Canonical inputs: `canonical/CANONICAL_DEFINITIONS.md` (tau=t+i psi, not necessarily periodic); `canonical/ACTION.en.md` (`DEFINED_FAMILY_NOT_FINALIZED`). Previous residual connection results #780, #781, #787. Conditional SO(2) action comparison #789 remains a draft.

## Questions with explicit tests
1. **Domain choice.** Is psi a noncompact scalar coordinate, a compact circle, or a complex-time field parameter? Do not silently identify these models. A circle requires independent topology and boundary assumptions.
2. **Vacuum definition.** A vacuum is a stationary solution to *one fixed action* satisfying constraints and boundary conditions, modulo diffeomorphisms, internal gauge equivalence, and permissible changes of representation. Kinematic solutions alone are not vacua.
3. **Holonomy.** On a noncontractible loop C, compare Wilson loops / conjugacy classes after verifying actual structure group, its gauge action, allowed bundles and whether the residual SO(2) connection is physical. The holonomy of a nontrivial cycle may be continuous; do not label it a quantized vacuum count absent proof.
4. **Stability.** Compute full second variation on the constraint-reduced physical phase space and boundedness; track negative-norm / ghost modes and nondegenerate Theta tetrads.
5. **Selection.** Require an energy criterion, topology quantization or independently derived transition rates from the same action. No anthropic or statistical counting without a well-defined measure.
6. **Phenomenology.** For any survivors compute spectra, couplings and at least one difference from existing field/string-theoretic predictions.

## String duality: strong, falsifiable meaning
An analogy between 10 real-dimensional coordinates or compact internal sectors **does not imply duality**. A candidate duality must identify a specified string theory/background and a mapped UBT sector, their degrees of freedom and gauge quotient, physical Hilbert spaces, operator dictionary, spectra, scattering/correlation functions, partition functions in a common regime, moduli/topological sectors, and anomaly constraints. At minimum find one nontrivial matched observable not fitted post hoc; disprove the correspondence if its spectrum or anomaly count differs. This is substantially stronger than agreement of low-energy Einstein equations.

## Minimal work package
- WP0: finalize full Theta action with real pairing, local measure including psi treatment, independent/composite connections, variation including boundary terms.
- WP1: compute Dirac primary/secondary constraints and their rank, gauge generators, physical Hessian and bounded Hamiltonian on nondegenerate backgrounds.
- WP2: only then enumerate inequivalent solutions and potential moduli/holonomies.
- WP3: compare a named dual candidate's protected quantities or controlled low-energy sectors; classify status as no dictionary / partial correspondence / conditional effective matching / demonstrated duality.

References: Douglas & Kachru, Rev. Mod. Phys. 79, 733 (2007), DOI 10.1103/RevModPhys.79.733 (flux vacuum landscape); Aharony et al., arXiv:hep-th/9905111 (precise AdS/CFT-type dictionaries). These references are comparison frameworks, not evidence for UBT duality.
