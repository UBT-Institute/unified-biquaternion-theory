# Local sectors versus Hamiltonian: exact separation

**Purpose:** prevent mixing a geometric connection test with a physical count of UBT vacua or particles.

For fixed tetrad and two Lorentz carriers, the conditional geometry leaves a stabilizer one-form alpha. Two distinct connections omega0 and omega0+alpha J can differ in curvature while satisfying the same two transport constraints. This is a counterexample to kinematic uniqueness, **not** proof of two on-shell vacua. An action can set alpha to zero, integrate it out, propagate it, or leave a gauge redundancy. No conclusion on the vacuum landscape follows without a selected action.

A fixed-background comparison with induced Abelian normal-plane connection A illustrates the canonical divide:

S_trial= integral d4x sqrt(-g) [-c/4 F_mu nu F^mu nu - (m²/2)(A_mu-B_mu)(A^mu-B^mu)].

This expression is a **diagnostic independent-connection theory**: mass sign and metric conventions require examination; B is an external one-form or a compensated composite. For c>0 in flat mostly-plus coordinates with B=0 and m²>0, the Maxwell kinetic gives canonical momenta pi^i=c F_0i and pi^0=0. The primary constraint pi^0=0 is a gauge constraint if m=0; for m>0 the A_0 equation supplies a second-class partner and three standard Proca polarizations. Neither result can be imported into composite UBT without the Theta chain rule and constraint analysis. c=0 removes time derivatives for A and typically makes the connection auxiliary.

**Most important action-selection test:** insert omega=omega0+alpha J directly into each *declared* Theta action family without adding arbitrary couplings. Compute the dependence of the resulting density on alpha and dot(alpha), then its EL equation and velocity Hessian, including any metric and auxiliary chain rule. If pure volume and the tetrad depends only on omega v, omega w, stabilizer C annihilates both carriers; alpha may be entirely invisible to that trial density. Then no local dynamics selects alpha. Curvature actions see alpha but require an independent derivation to be called fundamental.

CMB, particle spectra, alpha fine structure and cosmic constants must be postponed until a viable constrained action and background exist.

Status: methodology and conditional benchmark, not completed UBT action or Hamiltonian.
