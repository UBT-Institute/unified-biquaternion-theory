# Dirac–Kähler first-order closure test for the UBT Lorentz-vector carrier

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: working research, AI-assisted; no canonical claim promotion.

## Setup and independently assumed data

Let M be an oriented four-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian manifold with metric g and Levi–Civita connection. **These are background inputs for this test**, not derived here from UBT Theta. Identify the Lorentz-vector slice of Theta with a one-form V by the musical isomorphism. Let d be the exterior derivative and delta its metric formal adjoint (codifferential); in Lorentz signature formal adjointness is not a positive Hilbert-adjointness statement.

On the total exterior algebra Omega^*(M), define the Hodge–Dirac / Dirac–Kähler operator D_K=d+delta. The nilpotence d^2=delta^2=0 yields

  D_K^2 = d delta + delta d = Delta_H,

the Hodge–de Rham Laplacian, with conventional sign choices.

## Theorem 1: one-form non-closure and cyclic closure

On a generic one-form V,

  D_K V = delta V (0-form) + dV (2-form),

so Omega^1 is **not** preserved by D_K. The full unrestricted graded exterior bundle Omega^0⊕...⊕Omega^4 is preserved by D_K (16 real form components at a point in four dimensions). Treating all of them as independent propagating fields would be extra physics, not an automatic consequence of a single Theta.

However the **cyclic differential subspace** generated by a fixed V has a restricted form:

  D_K^{2n} V = Delta_H^n V is a 1-form,
  D_K^{2n+1} V = (d+delta) Delta_H^n V belongs to Omega^0⊕Omega^2.

Proof: D_K commutes with Delta_H and Delta_H preserves form degree. In addition, for J = D_K V one has d(dV)=0 and delta(delta V)=0, hence D_K J=delta dV + d delta V has degree 1 only, with no degree-3 component.

**Caveat:** This is closure of the differential orbit, not proof that an arbitrary independent 0+2 system forms a closed invariant off-shell subbundle. Allowing arbitrary two-forms generates 3-forms under d. Keeping J=DV is a differential (holonomic) constraint and needs a constraint/Hessian analysis before quantization.

## Theorem 2: curved-space operator carries Ricci curvature

For a one-form V_mu, in standard Hodge conventions the Weitzenbock identity gives

  (Delta_H V)_mu = -nabla^nu nabla_nu V_mu + Ric_mu{}^nu V_nu

in Riemannian signature (Lorentzian sign and operator conventions adjusted accordingly). Curvature is fixed in the square of the operator once g is supplied; it is **not** a first-principles derivation of the dynamical metric g or Einstein equations.

For the flat case this reduces to a component wave/Laplace operator on V. The decomposition

  Delta_H V = (delta d)V + (d delta)V

shows how a single geometrically defined operator combines transverse and longitudinal derivative structures with equal relative normalization **provided** D_K and the metric adjoint have been selected. Their selection is not forced by the vector carrier alone.

## Constraint / degree-count warning

At each spacetime point: dim Lambda^0=1, dim Lambda^1=4, dim Lambda^2=6, dim Lambda^3=4, dim Lambda^4=1. There is no license to call 16 components of the unconstrained total exterior algebra physical UBT particles. The constrained pair (V, J=DV) is not independent. Separately imposing a first-order evolution on (V,J) without specifying the action, symplectic structure or boundary conditions is not quantization.

## Consistency-first audit

1. The Lorentz-vector representation admits no linear first-order equivariant endomorphism with field-independent principal symbol (see lorentz_equivariant_vector_operators.md).
2. D_K evades that no-go by acting on a **graded representation**; the route V -> (0+2) -> V closes only on the holonomic orbit J=DV.
3. The background Hodge star uses g, so if g is composite g[DTheta], varying D_K must include variations of the star, delta and connection. Ignoring these terms is circular.
4. In a torsionful UBT branch, exterior d remains metric-independent on ordinary forms, while delta depends on the metric; replacing d by antisymmetrized torsionful covariant derivatives introduces torsion terms and is **not** ordinary exterior differentiation. Distinguish these constructions.
5. The squared Hodge–Dirac operator being of Laplace type after Euclidean continuation permits heat-kernel analysis conditionally, but leaves spectral test function, cutoff, physical measure, constraints and sign of induced gravity undetermined.
