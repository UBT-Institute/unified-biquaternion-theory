# Operator-first UBT: status, selection obstruction and attribution

**Status:** research only, Tier C, AI-assisted; no canonical promotion or peer review. The full UBT master equation and fundamental action remain OPEN.

## Established mathematical tools versus conditional UBT deductions

- **Standard mathematics, not a UBT novelty claim:** Pauli–Weyl factorization, the complex Clifford-algebra classification, Dirac–Kähler/Hodge calculus, and inner derivations of Mat_2(C).
- **Composite-coframe premise:** in the non-null Lorentz-vector branch let `D_mu V^a = s e_mu^a` with invertible tetrad `e` and induced metric `g`.
- **Local kernel:** for fixed `V` and `e`, shifts `omega -> omega+C` with `C_mu V=0` preserve `e`. The non-null Lorentz stabilizer has dimension 3 per spacetime direction, hence 12 algebraically undetermined connection components. These are not 12 physical particle modes.
- **Integrability:** `R_mu_nu{}^a{}_b V^b = s T_mu_nu^a` is a compatibility identity; by itself it does not select the connection.
- **Observer dependence:** permitted shifts preserve the metric but generally change torsion, connection curvature, holonomy and the second covariant derivative of `V`. A metric-only observable system cannot distinguish them; a full-connection theory can.
- **Conditional information-theory inference:** if a quantum scalar theory and its states use only `g`, their relative entropy is insensitive to `C` as well. This does not establish a fundamental UBT quantum theory or gravity dynamics.
