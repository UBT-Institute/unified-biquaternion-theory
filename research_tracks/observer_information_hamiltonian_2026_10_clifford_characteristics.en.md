# Clifford principal symbol and causal cone

<!-- BILINGUAL-UNIT: definition -->
## Derived characteristic identity

The canonical covariant tetrad and its existing block Clifford lift imply the identities below for the first-order Dirac-type *candidate* operator $\mathscr D=\Gamma^\mu\nabla_\mu$. The determinant sign is positive: in four complex dimensions the eigenvalues occur with even multiplicities; equivalently use the characteristic polynomial and the standard flat Lorentz frame. This requires a nondegenerate Lorentzian metric and a connection whose coefficients do not modify the principal symbol.

<!-- BILINGUAL-UNIT: math -->
## Calculation

$$\{\Gamma^\mu,\Gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}I_4,\qquad P(k)=\Gamma^\mu k_\mu.$$
$$P(k)^2=g^{\mu\nu}k_\mu k_\nu I_4,\qquad \det P(k)=(g^{\mu\nu}k_\mu k_\nu)^2.$$
$$g^{\mu\nu}k_\mu k_\nu=0.$$

## Physical scope

The null cone is a **conditional characteristic result** for this Dirac-type operator. It is not an unconditional proof that the full composite $\Theta$ equations have the same symbol, that all physical modes are hyperbolic, or that microcausality holds after quantization. Restore the measured speed $c$ through local frame units; this algebra alone does not derive its numerical SI value. No observer-capacity parameter appears in the characteristic polynomial: observer limitations constrain accessible measurements, not the cone, unless they modify the physical differential operator.
